## Research and proof discipline

KILL-FIRST remains a way to reject a false route early, not a mandate to produce
endless NO-GO notes. Preserve a useful mechanism from an incorrect sketch and
construct the missing implication with named inputs and an actual consumer.
Lean: **classify once, specialize late**. Narrow tests during development;
complete applicable checks at acceptance. Never replace expected evidence solely
because a replay failed.

Progress means a load-bearing premise removed or a proved bridge actually used
in the capstone. Record proved/published/replayed/accepted/consumed separately.
No current handoff document certifies full GR. No endpoint average silently
replaces the original raw source norm, and no source is fitted to the solution.
