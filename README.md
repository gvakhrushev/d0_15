## Scientific direction and current boundary

```text
reproducible distinction / no obligatory external catalogue
  -> native records, comparisons and minimal closure
  -> one physically admitted action and its complete joint variation
  -> exact joint states and controlled refinement
  -> nonlinear geometric equation and independently owned matter source
```

There are two coupled fronts: **native foundations → action/source**, and **exact geometric sector → nonlinear joint limit**. One strong integrator must join them during the work. Bounded agents supply specific missing inputs; they do not independently choose different actions, traces, preparation rules or norms.

Preserve the useful mechanisms behind φ, π₀, the stable flat reference, resonant matter and the AF/archive process. Do not turn spectral multiplicities into angles, fitted coincidences into premises, a finite root into an all-mesh theorem, or a linear Ward identity into nonlinear diffeomorphism invariance. A change is progress when it removes a load-bearing premise or is actually consumed by the shared theorem.
