## Why delegated

This is a bounded exact cyclotomic certificate on two already owned singular
physical orbit types. It is independently reviewable and settles whether the
moving-germ detune forcing lies in the physical conjugate-paired image. Its
result is not determined by a rank table or the holomorphic identity, so an
explicit exact certificate is preferable to another numerical residual.

## Objective

Set

$$
d_r(z)=z_r^{-1}-1,\qquad q_0(z)=d(z)d(z)^T,\qquad
w_j(z)=(z_j\partial_{z_j}C(z))\,\operatorname{vec}_{\rm sym}(q_0(z)).
$$

Use the #216/#262 physical convention

$$
P(z)=[A(z)\mid C(\bar z)],
$$

with $A(z)=H_{AB}(z)^T$ and $C(z)=H_{AQ}(z)$. The transpose in $q_0=dd^T$
is algebraic, not Hermitian. The phase-tangent forcing is $i w_j$, so it has
the same exact image-membership status.

At the exact characters
$z_5=(i,i,-i,-i)$ (orbit 5) and $z_7=(-1,i,i,-1)$ (orbit 7), for every
$j=0,1,2,3$:

1. compute exact ranks of $P(z)$ and its augmentation by $w_j$ over
   $\mathbb Q(i)$;
2. state whether $w_j$ has zero class in the physical cokernel;
3. compute an exact Hermitian residual norm in the owner pairing, reporting
   both raw $q_0$ and unit-normalized $q_0$ conventions;
4. independently check the submitted orbit-5 split (directions 0,1 outside,
   directions 2,3 inside) and classify orbit 7 without assuming the same
   pattern.

Use exact rational/cyclotomic arithmetic throughout. The certificate must
not use floating point, SVD thresholds, or a pseudoinverse. Include the
holomorphic $[A(z)\mid C(z)]$ result only as a labelled contrast.

## Terminal and stopping rule

The certificate passes only if all eight membership decisions and exact
residuals reproduce from the merged owners. A mismatch with the submitted
$\sqrt{8/5}$ residual must be explained by an exact normalization or pairing
conversion, not patched by a tolerance. This finite symbol result does not
prove that a forcing is generated by a nonlinear solution and does not settle
the stress task.
