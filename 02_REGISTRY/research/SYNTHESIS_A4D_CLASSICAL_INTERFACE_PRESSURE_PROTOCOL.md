A seam is accepted only when:

1. its exact type is fixed;
2. necessity and sufficiency are separated;
3. nearest counterexamples are owned;
4. rank-degenerate cases are owned;
5. labelled-holonomy cases are owned;
6. Lean owns every theorem-ready algebraic part;
7. no stronger classical claim is made than the literal hypotheses support.

The objective is not to reach the classical interface quickly.

The objective is to make every surviving interface difficult to break.
