## 2. Classify every relative QUERY critical point

Use the orthogonal block decomposition P plus I-P:

    T=[ A  B* ; B  D ], B*B=I-A^2.

For an arbitrary anti-Hermitian generator X, a relative QUERY variation is

    dJ=-2z Tr_r(R A dA)
       =-2z Tr([T,W]X), W=P R A P.                    (2)

The trace is real. Since [T,W] is anti-Hermitian, this vanishes for all X iff
[T,W]=0. Its off-diagonal block is B R A. R commutes with A and is invertible;
therefore

    d_QUERY J=0 on the whole reflection orbit
      iff B A=0
      iff A^2(I-A^2)=0
      iff spec(A) is contained in {-1,0,+1}.          (3)

The middle equivalence follows by taking (BA)*(BA)=A^2(I-A^2), a positive
matrix. This proves necessity as well as the previously known sufficiency
of projection-valued escape F=I-A^2. No numerical rank census is used.

## 3. Moving the cut does not erase the relative equation

The simultaneous transformation (P,T)->(VPV*,VTV*) leaves (1) invariant.
For a genuine moving P, K_P=[dP,P] obeys [K_P,P]=dP. Consequently the exact
relative source is obtained by replacing dT with

    dT_rel=dT-[K_P,T].                                (6)

This is the already owned moving-cut calculus applied to (1), not freezing P.
Under diagonal co-conjugation generated by X, the canonical choice K_P gives
dT_rel=[X-K_P,T], not necessarily zero. The difference X-K_P commutes with P,
so its compression derivative is a commutator with A and its trace-log

add a block-diagonal transport. For example [X,P]=0 may coexist with
[X,T]!=0, so canonical relative velocity must not be called identically zero.
In either transport the full covector does not vanish: the fixed-P relative
direction (4) remains unless the actual domain excludes it. Changing
coordinates or quotienting the diagonal action cannot turn a noncritical
orbit into a critical one.

If states and all marked effects/prefix tests are also co-transported, then
Tr(V rho V* V E V*)=Tr(rho E) for every actual test. Such passive transport
has zero derivative in the operational quotient. If laboratory marks are
