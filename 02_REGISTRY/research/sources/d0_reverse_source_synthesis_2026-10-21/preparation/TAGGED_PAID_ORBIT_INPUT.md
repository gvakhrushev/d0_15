## 3. Paid Bell processing, successful factorization and every failure

 K_{P,k}=U_kP/d,\qquad K_{P,k}^*K_{P,k}=I/d^2.               \tag{4}

\[
 |\Psi_k\rangle
    =\sum_t\sqrt{p_t}\,|t\rangle_{T_E}|t\rangle_Q
                           |\zeta_{t,k}\rangle.           \tag{8}
\]

The complete information cut in this adapter is E_total=T_E plus all prefix,
unused-bank, controller and original downstream record work. Its complement is

\[

   =H_\phi(p_t)+\sum_{t\notin\mathrm{ready}}p_t S_\phi(E_t)
