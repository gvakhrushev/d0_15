## Exact scope of the composition

- The Golden probes reconstruct real effect matrices if the required full
  original input contexts and cut measurements are available. They do not
  grant process tomography from one prepared state or arbitrary complex
  probes from a real gate grammar. Cost/cut commutation at only one anchor
  does not justify differentiating a joint classical record.
- The minimal-algebra necessity is for arbitrary independent Hermitian
  effect jets, within fixed unital complex *-algebra interfaces. A restricted
  native tangent space can have a smaller sufficient interface. The actual
  CAR parity reduction retains both blocks, all ports and original RIGHT.
- The paid adapter starts from common supplied `C0`, generates finite real
  orbit inputs, retains all failures and pays for a genuine output copy of
  reported prefix labels. Its affine price identity is on that declared
  enlarged tagged-E cut, for one fixed catalogue and specified additive
  counted ledger. It does not identify the unmodified Book03 price.
- Neither source sufficiency nor equality of J controls every retained
  entropy/clock term or the Hessian of a complete apparatus price. The
  full native controller, physical cut/valuation, independent equations and
  source-normalized refinement remain the parent gate.
