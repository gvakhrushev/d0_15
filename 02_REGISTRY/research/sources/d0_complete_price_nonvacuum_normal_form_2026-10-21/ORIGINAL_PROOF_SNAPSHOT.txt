## 1. The positive extension

The published
[complete-compiler coframe equivalence](sources/d0_native_gr_completion_synthesis_2026-10-20/book_source/COMPLETE_COMPILER_COFRAME_EQUIVALENCE.md)
removes the nonlinear scalar curvature coefficient in the vacuum price by
a positive radial change of coframe. Its matter obstruction is genuine:
the original nondensitized kinetic density scales with the opposite
degree, so one radial scale cannot in general turn the entire two-density
price into a prescribed linear combination of both densities.

The extension proved here retains that opposite degree and the entire
price. An exact algebraic stationary representation supplies a second
pair of coordinates at each free coframe site. After rescaling the
coframe, these coordinates have a unique stationary elimination. The
result is

\[
\boxed{
 S_{\mathrm{eff}}
 =B_{\mathrm{tot}}+\sum_{i\in I}a_iX_i+K(\mathbf Y;\zeta).
}
\tag{1}
\]

Here \(X_i\) is the same geometric density, \(Y_i\) is the same
nondensitized matter density, and \(K\) is determined by the actual
complete price. All its higher terms remain present. For a correlated
price, \(K\) may couple different sites; no spatial locality is inferred.

Three consequences are proved below.

1. The original finite action and (1) have corresponding full stationary
   systems near their common zero-density stratum. The construction
   retains the independent coframe, shared-link, matter and internal
   sources, the original measure and the actual held collar.
2. For the native one-stage CQ valuation, the first nonlinear matter
   coefficient satisfies \(K''(0)>0\). The previous one-scale obstruction
   becomes a derived nonlinear kinetic term with a fixed coefficient.
3. For every fixed finite cap admitted by the owned unit-charge
   interface, the curvature slope is nonzero. Its exact matter/curvature
   ratio is cap-independent and has an explicit finite-detector
   correction to \(10+6\sqrt5\).

The first statement is **stationary equivalence through an extended
function**. It is not a bare field-only off-shell identity. No additional
physical field, information functional, paid primitive or adjustable
compensator is introduced.

Let \(I\) be a finite set of **actually free** coframe sites. At each
\(i\in I\), \(e_i\) has its original sixteen independent components in an
open nondegenerate chart. The proper-Lorentz links \(U\), including every
link shared by based faces, remain independent. The original matter
coordinates are denoted by \(\Psi\), and other admitted independent
coordinates by \(\zeta\). Local charts may be used for the link group and
other manifolds. No radial coframe constraint is imposed on a free site.

Coframes on a held collar are fixed and are **never rescaled**. Only
free sites receive auxiliary coordinates. The admitted local domain is
a product in these free coframe directions and the remaining admitted
variables. Additional constraints that mix a free coframe with other
variables require their own domain analysis; unrestricted stationary
auxiliary elimination is not asserted for arbitrary coupled constraints.

Write the original free-site queries as

\[
 x_i=x_i(e_I,U,\Psi;\zeta),\qquad
 y_i=y_i(e_I,U,\Psi;\zeta).
\]

The essential carrier hypothesis is the following **independent
sitewise reciprocal homogeneity**: for independent positive \(u_j\)
near one, with the collar and all other variables held fixed,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 x_i((\sqrt{u_j}e_j)_{j\in I},U,\Psi;\zeta)
   &=u_i x_i(e_I,U,\Psi;\zeta),\\
 y_i((\sqrt{u_j}e_j)_{j\in I},U,\Psi;\zeta)
   &=u_i^{-1}y_i(e_I,U,\Psi;\zeta).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]

Global homogeneity under a common \(u_j=u\) is insufficient. In the
native application, the first identity is the local quadratic coframe
degree of the six-face geometric density; the second must be the
inverse-metric degree of the **original nondensitized** matter query.
Neighboring matter values in a difference stencil are allowed, as are
shared links, provided the actual coframe dependence obeys (2).
Multiplying the matter query by a determinant of the coframe changes this
interface and is not part of the theorem.

The actual declared finite ledger must have the form

\[
 S=B(\zeta)+S_{\mathrm{coll}}(U,\Psi,\zeta)
        +F(\mathbf x,\mathbf y;\zeta),
 \qquad F(0,0;\zeta)=0.
\tag{3}
\]

The function \(F\) may be an arbitrary smooth finite coupled function on
its declared record-support chart. Analytic input gives an analytic
normal form. All original fixed site weights can be included in \(F\);
moving weights retain their \(\zeta\)-derivatives. Normalization in (3)
means that the actual value \(F(0,0;\zeta)\) has been transferred into
\(B\), with its entire derivative, rather than discarded.

The spectator \(S_{\mathrm{coll}}\) denotes any retained collar price
that is independent of the free coframes. It may still have nonzero
shared-link, matter-stencil and internal derivatives. Independence is a
hypothesis about the actual support, for example a buffered local
stencil; the word “collar” alone does not establish it. Set

\[
B_{\mathrm{tot}}=B+S_{\mathrm{coll}},\qquad
a_i(\zeta)=F_{x_i}(0,0;\zeta),\qquad
b_i(\zeta)=F_{y_i}(0,0;\zeta),
\tag{4}
\]

and assume \(a_i\ne0\) at each free site. Shrink the chart if necessary
so their signs are fixed.

No assumption is made that \((\mathbf x,\mathbf y)\) are independent
coordinates on field space. In particular, the theorem remains valid
at a critical or rank-deficient zero-density dictionary. It is proved
on the original coframe/link/matter carrier, not by declaring its query
densities to be free field coordinates.

## 3. Exact algebraic representation and the coframe map

Introduce two real auxiliary coordinates \((s_i,\pi_i)\) at each
\(i\in I\), independent of all original variables. Define

\[
\widehat S
 =B_{\mathrm{tot}}+F(\mathbf s,\mathbf y;\zeta)
      +\sum_{i\in I}\pi_i(x_i-s_i).
\tag{5}
\]

Their equations, in the original coframe chart, are exactly

\[
s_i=x_i,\qquad
\pi_i=F_{x_i}(\mathbf x,\mathbf y;\zeta).
\tag{6}
\]

Substitution returns (3), including its baseline and every derivative.
Consequently original critical points lift uniquely to critical points
of (5), and every critical point of (5) projects to one of (3).
Equation (5) is an algebraic generating family for the same finite
function. It uses neither a convex Legendre transform nor an inverse of
the physical or preparation Hessian.

On the open chart \(\pi_i/a_i>0\), define only at free sites

\[
\widetilde e_i=\sqrt{\pi_i/a_i(\zeta)}\,e_i.
\tag{7}
\]

Leave the held collar, \(U,\Psi,\mathbf s,\boldsymbol\pi,\zeta\)
unchanged. Before auxiliary elimination this is an explicit
diffeomorphism. Its inverse is
\(e_i=\sqrt{a_i/\pi_i}\,\widetilde e_i\), and the determinant of
its sixteen-component coframe block is \((\pi_i/a_i)^8\). The whole
Jacobian is block triangular, so derivatives of \(a_i(\zeta)\) do not
spoil invertibility; they must be retained in source transport.

Let

\[
X_i=x_i(\widetilde e_I,U,\Psi;\zeta),\qquad
Y_i=y_i(\widetilde e_I,U,\Psi;\zeta).
\]

By (2), \(x_i=(a_i/\pi_i)X_i\) and
\(y_i=(\pi_i/a_i)Y_i\). Therefore (5) has the exact extended-function
identity

\[
\widehat S
 =B_{\mathrm{tot}}+\sum_i a_iX_i
       +G(\mathbf s,\boldsymbol\pi,\mathbf Y;\zeta),
\tag{8}
\]

where

\[
G(\mathbf s,\boldsymbol\pi,\mathbf Y;\zeta)
 =F\!\left(\mathbf s,
       ((\pi_i/a_i)Y_i)_i;\zeta\right)-\sum_i\pi_i s_i.
\tag{9}
\]

This calculation does not use a matter connection, a rescaling of
\(\Psi\), a determinant density or an alteration of the lattice
measure. In particular, the geometric term remains the original
weighted Palatini density under the original fixed sum or measure.
With shared links, its derivatives are the full derivatives of all
incident based terms, not separately chosen face derivatives.

## 4. The universal auxiliary block

Write \(Z_i=(\pi_i/a_i)Y_i\). The auxiliary equations in (8) are

\[
 F_{x_i}(\mathbf s,\mathbf Z;\zeta)-\pi_i=0,
 \qquad
 (Y_i/a_i)F_{y_i}(\mathbf s,\mathbf Z;\zeta)-s_i=0.
\tag{10}
\]

At \(\mathbf Y=0\), their root is exactly
\(\mathbf s=0,\boldsymbol\pi=\mathbf a\). Let \(n=|I|\) and evaluate
the following derivatives at \((\mathbf x,\mathbf y)=(0,0)\). In
the ordered auxiliary coordinates \((\mathbf s,\boldsymbol\pi)\),

\[
H_{\mathrm{aux}}
 =\begin{pmatrix}F_{xx}&-I_n\\-I_n&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
H_{\mathrm{aux}}^{-1}
 =\begin{pmatrix}0&-I_n\\-I_n&-F_{xx}\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{11}
\]

Multiplication gives the identity matrix. Its determinant is
\((-1)^n\), and its inertia is \((n,n,0)\). For example, the change
\(\delta\pi\mapsto\delta\pi-\tfrac12F_{xx}\delta s\) reduces its
quadratic form to \(-2\delta s^{T}\delta\pi\), displaying the
hyperbolic pairs. Thus the block is invertible for every symmetric
\(F_{xx}\), including zero and singular matrices.

The implicit-function theorem gives a unique nearby smooth section

\[
\mathbf s=\mathbf s(\mathbf Y;\zeta),\qquad
\boldsymbol\pi=\boldsymbol\pi(\mathbf Y;\zeta),\qquad
(\mathbf s(0),\boldsymbol\pi(0))=(0,\mathbf a).
\tag{12}
\]

Define

\[
K(\mathbf Y;\zeta)
 =G(\mathbf s(\mathbf Y;\zeta),
      \boldsymbol\pi(\mathbf Y;\zeta),\mathbf Y;\zeta).
\tag{13}
\]

Substituting this section in (8) gives (1). Since the auxiliary first
derivatives vanish, differentiating the reduced function retains every
original field and internal equation by the stationary envelope
identity. Together with (5)--(8), this proves the following statement.

> **Finite coupled-price stationary normal-form theorem.** Under
> (2)--(4), on the product domain of actually free coframes and the
> remaining admitted variables, the original action (3) and (1) have
> locally corresponding full critical sets near their common
> zero-density stratum. The correspondence is exact, uses the unique
> algebraic section (12), and preserves the held collar. It does not
> require a positive or invertible physical Hessian, a nondegenerate
> preparation Hessian, an invertible density dictionary, or a factorized
> history price.

The theorem is local in the real field chart and the fixed support of
the declared finite ledger. It does not infer uniform neighborhoods in
the cap or lattice size.

There is a direct reverse-engineering interpretation. A free radial
variation \(e_i\mapsto e^{t/2}e_i\) in the original action has the
Euler trace

\[
\frac{dS}{dt}\bigg|_{t=0}=x_iF_{x_i}-y_iF_{y_i}.
\tag{12a}
\]

At a full critical point it vanishes. The new auxiliary equations give
\(s_i\pi_i=(\pi_iY_i/a_i)F_{y_i}\), which is exactly this trace
relation when pulled back by (6)--(7). Near zero its first-order
relation is \(\delta x_i=(b_i/a_i)\delta y_i\). This is the direction
appearing in the derived matter Hessian below. Thus the auxiliary
section expresses a trace relation already required by the original
free coframe equations; it does not supply a new physical primitive.
The generating-family and stationary-elimination method is classical;
the present result is its application to the owned complete native
price and reciprocal coframe degrees.

### 4.1 Why this is not a bare off-shell equality

Equations (5) and (8) are the same **extended** function in different
coordinates. Eliminating the auxiliaries at fixed original coframes
gives (3); eliminating them at fixed transformed coframes gives (1).
Those two operations do not commute off shell, because (7) mixes a
coframe with \(\pi\). A simple identity of the form
\(S=S_{\mathrm{eff}}\circ\Phi\) on the original field space has not
been proved or used.

At a full critical point, ordinary coordinate invariance of the
differential and stationary auxiliary elimination give the claimed
bijection. This is the precise reason the previous field-only radial
obstruction does not exclude the present result.

### 4.2 Coupled collar records and other spectator parameters

The main statement treats an explicit coframe-independent collar
spectator and independent parameters \(\zeta\). If the actual price
instead has a correlated dependence
\(F(\mathbf x_I,\mathbf y_I;w(U,\Psi,\zeta),\zeta)\), the same
algebra can be applied with \(w\) as a parameter and then pulled back
to its actual value, provided it is independent of the free coframes.
The coordinate map remains block triangular in the unscaled variables.
All \(Dw\) terms must be retained, and now
\(a_i=a_i(w(U,\Psi,\zeta),\zeta)\) can vary with links and matter.
The resulting curvature term is \(\sum_i a_i(w,\zeta)X_i\); it is
not a Palatini term with a constant coefficient under those variations.
This extension must not be cited as an automatic match to a
constant-coupling Einstein action.

If a supposed collar term actually depends on free coframes, it cannot
be declared a spectator. It must be included in a ledger obeying the
required homogeneities, or the theorem's carrier hypothesis fails.

## 5. The derived nonlinear matter jet

At fixed \(\zeta\), put

\[
D=\operatorname{diag}(b_i/a_i).
\]

Differentiating (10) at \(\mathbf Y=0\) gives

\[
 D_Y\mathbf s(0)=D,\qquad
 D_Y\boldsymbol\pi(0)=F_{xx}D+F_{xy}.
\tag{14}
\]

The envelope identity applied to (13) yields

\[
K(0;\zeta)=0,\qquad K_Y(0;\zeta)=\mathbf b,
\tag{15}
\]

and the exact coupled Hessian

\[
\boxed{
 K_{YY}(0)
 =F_{yy}+D F_{xy}+F_{yx}D+D F_{xx}D.
}
\tag{16}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
v^TK_{YY}(0)v
 =\begin{pmatrix}Dv\\v\end{pmatrix}^{T}
  H_F(0,0)
  \begin{pmatrix}Dv\\v\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{17}
\]

These formulas give the actual higher response rather than deleting it.
They also display its possible coupling between sites. Only when the
declared price factorizes appropriately, for example
\(F=\sum_i m_iF_i(x_i,y_i;\zeta)\) with the actual fixed weights,
does the elimination factorize as \(K=\sum_i m_iK_i(Y_i;\zeta)\).

would additionally require the appropriate measure and continuum

For one pair, write \(a=F_x(0,0)\), \(b=F_y(0,0)\). Formula (16)

\[
\boxed{
K''(0)=F_{yy}+2(b/a)F_{xy}+(b/a)^2F_{xx}.

\tag{18}

## 7. Exact null-matter persistence and the full internal source

By (15)--(16), on a sufficiently small chart,

\[
K(\mathbf Y;\zeta)
 =\sum_i b_i(\zeta)Y_i+O(\|\mathbf Y\|^2).
\tag{33}
\]

The remainder has zero value and zero full first differential on
\(\mathbf Y=0\). On the common null stratum
\(\mathbf X=\mathbf Y=0\), the auxiliary section has
\(\mathbf s=0,\boldsymbol\pi=\mathbf a\), and the free-coframe map
(7) is the identity. The full first source is exactly

\[
\boxed{
dS_{\mathrm{eff}}\big|_{X=Y=0}
 =dB_{\mathrm{tot}}+\sum_i(a_i\,dX_i+b_i\,dY_i).
}
\tag{34}
\]

This is the source of the actual linear action

\[
S_{\mathrm{lin}}
 =B_{\mathrm{tot}}+\sum_i(a_iX_i+b_iY_i)
\tag{35}
\]

at that stratum. The assertion includes ambient variations for which
\(dY_i\ne0\). A nonzero, time-dependent null material field may
have \(Y_i=0\) while its stress source \(dY_i\) is nonzero; no
variation is discarded by imposing a null ansatz before differentiating.

Consequently every full critical configuration of (35) on this
stratum is an exact full critical configuration of the original
finite-price action (3), and conversely locally, with the same held
collar and admitted variables. There is no truncation in the resulting
stationary statement. The higher terms of \(K\) vanish only to first
order on this stratum, rather than being erased from the action.

The positive content beyond an isolated first-source calculation is
the entire nearby stationary representation (1), its unique algebraic
section, and its fixed off-stratum matter response (16). It identifies
where the complete higher price goes.

\tag{36}
\]

Terms \((D_\zeta a_i)X_i\) and \((D_\zeta b_i)Y_i\) vanish at
