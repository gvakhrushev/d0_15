# Literature context: Palatini correspondence and the scope of this result

21 October 2026. Narrow primary-source check; this is not a priority survey.

## Two directly relevant primary papers

1. V. I. Afonso, G. J. Olmo and D. Rubiera-Garcia,
   *Mapping Ricci-based theories of gravity into general relativity*,
   Physical Review D **97**, 021503(R) (2018).
   [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.97.021503).
   This establishes a correspondence between solution spaces of a class
   of metric-affine Ricci-based theories and GR.

2. V. I. Afonso, G. J. Olmo, E. Orazi and D. Rubiera-Garcia,
   *Correspondence between modified gravity and general relativity with scalar fields*,
   Physical Review D **99**, 044040 (2019).
   [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.99.044040);
   [author manuscript, arXiv:1810.04239v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.04239v2).
   Sections II–III use covariant metric-affine actions and an auxiliary
   metric; the scalar Lagrangian changes under the correspondence.
   In the Palatini f(R) case the metric relation is q_munu=f_R g_munu.
   Section III.2, equations (28)–(33), explicitly maps quadratic
   f(R)=R+alpha R^2 with a free canonical scalar to the Einstein-frame
   scalar function K(Z)=Z-alpha kappa^2 Z^2, in the authors' conventions.
   Their introduction, footnote 1, corrects an earlier omission of the
   nonlinear scalar transformation: the earlier associated scalar
   solutions were approximations, not exact solutions. Use the 2019
   treatment for this matter-sector comparison.

## The measure boundary remains essential

The cited papers start from generally covariant actions with metric
volume factors; the repository construction retains its given lattice
measure and nondensitized q_mat. A fixed-measure term K(q_mat) has not
thereby become sqrt(-g) P(X). The coframe homogeneities, independent
Lorentz-link variables, complete source transport and boundary data must
be checked in the repository's own variables.

Thus the justified description is an exact local stationary normal form
with a derived nonlinear kinetic functional, conceptually related to
Palatini matter correspondences. A covariant Einstein-frame or physical
k-essence identification requires additional density, continuum and
admission arguments. The literature supplies neither Book Info nor a
physical implementation of our auxiliaries, and it does not make the
remaining D0-to-physical-GR closure automatic.
