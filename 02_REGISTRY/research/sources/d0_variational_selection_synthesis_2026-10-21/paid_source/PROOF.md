### Exact boundary concerning the instruction controller

The source explicitly distinguishes the following three claims.

1. **Prospective action-word interface:** the AB/BA alternatives are charged
   executable words on the same declared quantum blanks. If this is the
   admissible comparison class, (4) excludes the centre word as its minimum.
2. **One fixed autonomous controller:** the writer's separate §9 instrument
   reaches every row from the same complete initial controller, but with
   successful probabilities `gamma/M`. Its full price is
   `B_uniform-gamma[log_phi M+(1/M)sum F_h(k)]`, not any conditional `S_m(k)`.
   Reading a low-price branch does not replace that full averaged price.
3. **Physical G0 instruction selection:** if the instruction word is stored
   in the initial controller, distinct prescribed words have distinct
   controller inputs. Their preparation/selection is not solved by putting
   code bits into gate order. No compulsory autonomous choice law is proved.

Nor does (4) compare the best separately optimized compiler for the centre
against the best independently optimized compiler for the competitor: their
baseline costs may differ. A finite discrete point is topologically isolated,
so this is not called a failure of a topological local minimum on a fixed
finite code set. It is the explicit strict three-node price comparison.

### Conditional Gibbs corollary, with the missing premise displayed

If the same actual complete policy execution has code law `pi`, pure
conditional retained work, a code-independent paid baseline and `n`
conditionally independent report replicas while the code is read once, its
full counted-CQ price is

\[
S_n(\pi)=B+\gamma\{n\sum_k\pi_kP(c_k)-H_\varphi(\pi)\}.
\]

The standard finite-dimensional entropy calculation then gives

\[
\pi_k^*=\frac{\varphi^{-nP(c_k)}}{\sum_j\varphi^{-nP(c_j)}},\qquad
\frac{\pi^*_{\rm cheaper}}{\pi^*_0}
=\varphi^{n(P(c_*)-P(c_{\rm cheaper}))}>1.
\]

The common baseline cancels in the ratio. This is a consequence **if the
full paid policy family and its optimization are admitted**. The current
uniform controller does not supply that optimization; finite `Q(p)` row
preparation does not assert that the generally transcendental Gibbs optimum
is exactly attainable. No physical Book03 law is imported by this corollary.

## 2. Positive weak-source result: PF jumps do not destroy dual-norm consistency

Consume the same-price rank-two source owner, specifically
`sources/d0_star_rank2_seam_2026-10-21/check_global_star_pf_jump.py` and its
original material current. The rank-two finite representative has `q_h=0`,
while its true six-face star density obeys `||f_h||_infinity<=C_f h` on a
compact smooth slab. Its special `r=1` cell has a stronger cancellation;
the general all-time statement used here is only `O(h)`.

Keep exactly the canonical/null pullback `F_cart(x,y)=F_null(-x,y)`, the
same sigma-minus detector, and the source normalization

\[
a=F_x(0,0)\ne0,\qquad
k_z=-\frac{F_y(-\tau f_{h,z}/\rho_{h,z},0)}{a},\qquad
k_0=-\frac{F_y(0,0)}{a}.                                  \tag{5}
\]

These are the very slopes used in the PF-jump checker; no new price or
normalization is introduced. On a compact strict operand interval let
`L= sup |F_yx(x,0)/a|<infinity`. Then

\[
|k_z-k_0|\le L\tau|f_{h,z}|/\rho_{h,z}.                    \tag{6}
\]

For either genuine scalar A let `V^r_{A,z}=g_h^{rs}(z)D_s^+psi_A(z)` be
the actual discrete material current before multiplication by k. The
nonlinear-minus-affine **normalized material Euler source** is exactly

\[
R_{A,h}=-2\sum_rD_r^-[(k-k_0)V_A^r].                       \tag{7}
\]

The factor 2 and actual shifts are retained. This follows by differentiating
the original quadratic q, not by assuming the nonlinear scalar equation
vanishes. If the affine source has a separate error it must be added to (7).

Give the finite lattice its physical measure `h^d sum_z` and test only
scalar variations vanishing on the same full collar (or an explicitly
periodic regulator). Discrete summation by parts, with every boundary term
therefore zero, gives

\[
\langle R_{A,h},v\rangle_h
=2\langle(k-k_0)V_A,\nabla_h^+v\rangle_h.
\]

Define the discrete negative norm as the dual of the gradient seminorm on
that test space; in the periodic case use mean-zero tests, equivalently
the quotient by constants. Hence

\[
\boxed{\|R_{A,h}\|_{-1,h}
\le2\|(k-k_0)V_A\|_{2,h}
\le2L\tau\|(f_h/\rho_h)V_A\|_{2,h}.}                    \tag{8}
\]

In particular, if `rho_h>=rho_min>0`, `||V_A||_{2,h}<=C_V` uniformly and
`||f_h||_infinity<=C_fh`, then

\[
\|R_{A,h}\|_{-1,h}\le
2L\tau C_fC_V\rho_{\min}^{-1}h.                           \tag{9}
\]

There is **no derivative or neighbor-difference assumption on rho**.
Bounded order-one jumps at every edge are allowed. The earlier original-cell
discrepancy theorem already supplied an eventual uniform lower bound for
the actual normalized AF/PF density on a chosen Golden depth schedule;
this packet's original checker did not consume that application. The
[late native-PF application](../late_application/NATIVE_PF_AND_CLOCK_APPLICATION.md)
now consumes it with an explicit all-scale bound and the estimate (8).
For an unspecified or physically selected schedule the lower bound remains
an application requirement; (8) retains the general weighted condition.

### Sharp compatibility with the existing strong obstruction

On an even one-dimensional lattice on the unit periodic interval, `n h=1`,
let the two actual slopes
alternate between `k_a` and `k_b` and take `V=1`. Equation (7) has
`||R||_2=2|k_a-k_b|/h`. Its only Fourier mode is the Nyquist mode, whose
negative-Laplacian eigenvalue is `4/h^2`; therefore

\[
\|R\|_{-1,h}=|k_a-k_b|.
\]

For the actual nonlinear slopes in (5), constant `f_h=C h` and alternating
PF values 1 and 2 give `k_a-k_b=c h+O(h^2)` with generically `c!=0`.
Consequently the strong residual tends to a nonzero constant while the
negative norm tends to zero linearly. The existing PF-jump control and
(9) are simultaneously true.

This is a positive weak-consistency input for a **whole-slab coupled
variational solver**. It is not a replacement of raw216's specified norm,
not a full42 inverse, not existence of exact finite roots, and not a proof
of strong source convergence. A usable correction theorem still needs
the actual gauge/boundary mixed stability estimate with this dual norm,
all other field rows, and the same admitted complete Book price.
