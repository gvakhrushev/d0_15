PROOF CHECK

1. Repeated projective covariance and naturality put every rescaled source
   at the origin in one fixed finite-dimensional tensor line. The nonzero
   quadratic flat limit fixes that line to the Einstein line. No bound or
   convergence assumption on the scale-factor product was used.
2. The Ward identity gives spatial constancy of the proportionality factor
   on the invertible-Einstein locus. This step alone does not prove that
   the factor is independent of the metric.
3. The cutoff metric agrees with adjacent normal contractions near two
   distinct points. At each fixed positive contraction scale, a sufficiently
   small change preserves Lorentz signature and invertibility of G on a
   compact connected bridge. Germ locality and spatial constancy equate
   the two factors. Local constancy along the contraction interval and the
   flat limit fix the coefficient to a. This is the key additional step.
4. Arbitrarily small compactly supported quadratic jet perturbations make
   G invertible at the chosen point because Ricci jets are freely adjustable
   and trace reversal is invertible in dimension d>=3. Smooth continuity
   then extends the identity to singular G and Ricci-flat points.
5. The supplemental scale-defect formula has the correct sign and scale
   power: the finite weighted sum telescopes to E(lambda^(2N)g)-E(g).
   Absolute summability bounds deviations but does not force them to zero.

ESSENTIAL LIMITS

The theorem does not derive any of its physical-source hypotheses merely
from closedness of a system. In particular a global history multiplier
Gamma[g]G is not generally germ-local. A total metric-plus-matter Ward
identity contains matter Euler rows; the gravitational off-shell identity
must be obtained legitimately. The nonzero coefficient must concern the
quadratic curvature jet, not merely a nonzero higher-derivative flat
response. R^2 Euler sources show this distinction is necessary. The theorem
relaxes knowledge of the scale magnitude, but does not by itself establish
the Cost-Info binding or actual admissible source descent.
