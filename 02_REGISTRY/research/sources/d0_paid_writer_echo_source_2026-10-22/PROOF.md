\[
 L_f\psi=\alpha_f\,(g\psi)\otimes w_f,
 \qquad \|w_f\|=1,\quad |\alpha_f|^2=w(f),                 \tag{1}
\]

up to a known transcript-wide phase. Both `w(f)` and `w_f` are independent
of the logical input, including external entanglement. The gate's canonical

needed; the isometry/effect identity `L_f* L_f=w(f)I` alone would not suffice.

Consequently the reversed **logical** word can be executed with fresh
factories on the same `B` wires. H, CNOT, Toffoli and controlled signs have

`G^-1=XGX`, where X is a CNOT from the retained known-one resource. The old

\[
  L^{\leftarrow}_{f}U\psi
   =\alpha_f\,\psi\otimes w^{\leftarrow}_{f}.             \tag{2}

All gates, resource banks, retries, actual clocks, readings and aborts of

 B_0=pI+p^2X,\quad B_1=-ap(I-X).

 K_+=B_0A_1=2apP_+,\quad K_-=B_1A_0=-2apP_-,\quad

 \boxed{B-\Gamma\left[\log_\varphi M+
