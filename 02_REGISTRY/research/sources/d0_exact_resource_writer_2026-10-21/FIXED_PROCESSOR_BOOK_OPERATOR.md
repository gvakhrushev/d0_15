## 2. All cost marks of the ACTUAL paid fixed processor

Let `K` be the complete input carrier of the published fixed processor,
including programme/material resources and retained purifiers. Its
complete coherent instrument is the fixed isometry

\[
 V=\bigoplus_f V_f:K\longrightarrow E,
 \qquad V_f^*V_f=w_f I_K,\quad\sum_f w_f=1.              \tag{1}
\]

Here `f` is the complete input-independent factory/outer transcript.
Within each branch `V_f`, every Bell/address/report outcome and quantum
work register is still present. The value `w_f` is state independent by
the actual factory theorem, including on entangled data; (1) is not
inferred merely from the unitary-programme section. Previously measured
permutation labels remain in this transcript also on a later abort.

The fixed schedule's actual charged processing cost is `c_f`: every
attempt, controlled gate, reference-report route and reading is charged
at its scheduled location, including zero-control cases. An early
factory abort can change `c_f`; it does not make it input dependent.
This is precisely the published supplied counted-cost interface, not
the unknown upstream state-writing or complete Book Info valuation.

For an analytical cost mark `t`, define

\[
 V_t=\bigoplus_f e^{-t c_f/2}V_f,\qquad
 n(t)=\sum_f w_f e^{-t c_f}>0.                            \tag{2}
\]

**Theorem 1.** On the whole full input carrier,

\[
 N_t=V_t^*V_t=n(t)I_K,\qquad
 (-\partial_t)^k N_t\big|_{t=0}
           =\Big(\sum_f w_f c_f^k\Big)I_K\quad(k\ge0).   \tag{3}
\]

Every normalized-input derivative of every cost moment and cumulant
therefore vanishes. For fixed `t`, the polar-normalized map
`Vhat_t=V_t/sqrt(n(t))` is a fixed isometry.

**Proof.** The record summands in (1) are orthogonal. Insert (1) into the
Gram product of (2); differentiate the finite sum. No branch is omitted,
no probability is conditioned on success and no state is reset.

This is stronger than the earlier mean-cost calculation and differs
from the original full128 mark, whose `N_n,t` is generally nonscalar.
The finite function `n(t)` is fixed by this processor's actual factory
law; no scalar is chosen to repair a source. A nonconstant physical
writer price is not included in `c_f` by convention.

Scalar `N_t` does **not** imply that the output range is independent of
the mark `t`: different factory blocks are reweighted on the output
side. Thus a genuine variation of `t`, charges or factory hardware can
change the normalized complement and must be retained. The claim is
zero input-state/encoded-field source at fixed hardware and mark.

## 3. Exact complete-writer pullback of the true Book complement

Let `T_y:H->K` be a genuinely supplied injective **complete** writer,
with `N_T=T_y^*T_y>0`. Its output includes every writer failure and its
retained work. At `t=0`, any fixed complete isometry may act conditionally
on that real writer status. At `t!=0`, the scalar marked substitution
below additionally requires every writer status to run the same padded
charged factory schedule, or a direct verification of `N_t=n(t)I` on the
enlarged input. Being one fixed complete isometry alone does not prove
this marked condition. If status-dependent skipping or early abort
changes the charged pattern, retain the actual `N_{V,t}=V_t^*V_t`:

\[
 \Pi_{V_tT}=V_tT(T^*N_{V,t}T)^{-1}T^*V_t^*.
\]

No scalar pullback is asserted in that case. These are interface
conditions, not an assertion that the required writer exists.
Let `i:H->E` be the
specified original isometric active embedding. Take `0<z<1` and `r>0`.
Dimensions, `i`, `z` and the mark
are fixed in the following formula.

Set `W_y=Vhat_t T_y`. Exact multiplication gives

\[
 \Pi_{W_y}=W_y(W_y^*W_y)^{-1}W_y^*
          =\widehat V_t\Pi_{T_y}\widehat V_t^*,
 \qquad \Pi_T=T(T^*T)^{-1}T^*.                            \tag{4}
\]

The same result follows by composing `V_t T_y` and retaining its actual
normalizer `n(t)N_T`. With the owner's actual reference multiplicity `r`
(omit it if already included in `H`), the normalized complement is

\[
 Q_y=(1-z)I_H+z i^*\widehat V_t\Pi_{T_y}\widehat V_t^*i,
 \qquad J_y=-r\log\det Q_y.                             \tag{5}
\]

**Theorem 2.** Its entire writer source is

\[
 DJ_y=-rz\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
 Q_y^{-1}i^*\widehat V_t(D\Pi_{T_y})\widehat V_t^*i
 \right].                                               \tag{6}
\]

All writer work, references and failures remain inside `T_y`. In
particular `DT=T X` has `D Pi_T=0`, and an invertible right-coordinate
change, including a common scalar, cannot create this source. A fixed
complete writer has zero source here. If an actual field writer changes
its range, (6) computes its genuine contribution rather than assuming it
away. Moving `i` adds the two cut terms in `D(i^* Pi_W i)`; moving `z`
or `Vhat_t` retains the corresponding product-rule terms.

For a rank-one prepared state `T_y(1)=C(y)`, `||C||=1`, (5) reduces to
the actual blank-return fidelity with the fixed vector
`b=Vhat_t^* i(1)`:

\[
 J_y=-r\log[1-z+z|\langle b,C(y)\rangle|^2].             \tag{7}
\]

This is an explicit full-operator preparation consumer, not a chosen
report entropy. It does not produce the required writer `T_y` from a
common native initial state.

If the entire input space is held active with fixed embedding and the
fields occur only in a supplied state `C(y)`, both `Vhat_t` and the
active cut are fixed: the complementary determinant has no such field
derivative. This does not make the full HJR price constant. Its genuine
heat operator and matter operator must still be identified, and a
Rayleigh term `Tr(rho Q)` retains `Tr(Q D rho)`. Choosing the report
effect as that `Q`, or making `C(y)` a new moving cut, needs the original
Book map; it is not a consequence of the CQ construction.

## 4. Explicit moving-programme cut in the complete logical Bell block

This section is a deliberately specified **logical Bell/address block**
of the native processor, conditional on its ideal logical-gate interface.
It is not its complete paid-H-factory corner. Factory ancillas, measured
teleportation inputs and their overlap with an actual active cut cannot
be assumed neutral when extending this calculation to the whole engine.

Use `d=32` in the native application, with `A` programme addresses. An
arbitrary normalized pure programme without an extra reference can be
written

\[
 |\Omega_S\rangle=(Ad)^{-1/2}\sum_{a,i}|a,i\rangle S_a|i\rangle,
 \qquad\sum_a\operatorname{Tr}(S_a^*S_a)=Ad.              \tag{8}
\]

An untouched reference can be included by giving `S_a` a larger output
space; all contractions below then use the same `S_a^*S_a`. Let the real
signed Pauli labels `ell` have identity label `0`. The actual Bell basis
change followed by copying the address and Bell computational values
to a fresh record gives

\[
 V_S\chi=\frac1{d\sqrt A}\sum_{a,\ell}
 |a,\ell\rangle_{Q,DP}|a,\ell\rangle_M
                 \otimes S_aP_\ell\chi.                  \tag{9}
\]

Every old `D,P,Q` register and the measured copy `M` is retained. Pauli
completeness implies `V_S^*V_S=I` for every normalized (8), not only
unitary programmes.

Consider exactly the initialized-input cut

\[
 i_S\chi=|\chi\rangle_D|\Omega_S\rangle_{QPB}|0\rangle_M,
 \qquad G_0=S_0^*S_0.                                   \tag{10}
\]

This is a moving cut constructed from the supplied programme. It is
not asserted to be the compulsory Book active cut. The record-zero
overlap in `i_S^*V_S` forces `a=ell=0`; the retained old `D,P` are then
both zero. Direct contraction with the **same** programme gives

\[
 \boxed{A_S=i_S^*V_S
     =\frac{|0\rangle\langle0|G_0}{A d^{3/2}}.}          \tag{11}
\]

Thus `A_S^*A_S` has one possible nonzero eigenvalue

\[
 \lambda_S=\frac{\langle0|G_0^2|0\rangle}{A^2d^3},
 \quad
 J_S=-r(d-1)\log(1-z)-r\log(1-z+z\lambda_S).             \tag{12}
\]

No scalar normalization is missing: (9) is already a complete isometry.
On every actual proper-unitary programme, `S_a=J_a(y)`, all `G_a=I`.
Then `A_S=|0><0|/(A d^(3/2))` and **all** proper-unitary field jets of
this Bell-block determinant vanish. Both the operation and moving-cut
derivatives have been included; freezing the programme bra in (11)
would give the wrong corner derivative.

On a normalized programme-normal direction at this section, however,

\[
 DJ_S=-\frac{2rz\langle0|DG_0|0\rangle}
        {A^2d^3(1-z+z/(A^2d^3))}.                        \tag{13}
\]

For `A>=2`, fix the actual primary unitaries `J_a` and use the exact
normalized curve
`S_0(u)=J_0 diag(sqrt(1+u),1,...)`,
`S_1(u)=J_1 diag(sqrt(1-u),1,...)`, with every other `S_a=J_a`. It has
`sum Tr G_a=Ad` and `DG_0=|0><0|` at `u=0`. Equation (13) is strictly
negative. This is a tangent admitted by the supplied arbitrary pure
programme interface; a physical writer for it is not inferred.

This computation therefore supplies an exact candidate cut test:
uniform unitary Bell labels do not determine its full feedback source,
and a genuine moving-input cut can add normal rows even where the CQ
construction cancels its own processing first source. It does not
prove the final paid engine has (11), nor that its full HJR normal rows
cannot receive true heat/matter/writer contributions. The rank-one
corner also cannot simply be renamed the original strictly positive
full-rank `B^n` without a different actual identification.
