  aggregate programme cannot be prepared from initial densities and raw
  primitive Kraus maps over `Q(sqrt(p),sqrt(2),i)`, already at one actual
  cost20/L4 primary point. A same-address coframe ratio has the forbidden
  square root of a rational number with odd 421 valuation. The pure-output
  result includes retained work and almost-surely finite halting. The
  separate mixed-probe exclusion is limited to finite execution trees.
- The same proof supplies the exact **fixed vacuum** Fourier128 material
  prefix in the declared addressed factory interface, with its full
  reference Gram and retained failures. It does not prepare the moving
  nonvacuum bank or prove physical primitive/blank admission.
- [Book operator proof](FIXED_PROCESSOR_BOOK_OPERATOR.md): the actual
  supplied processor has scalar full cost-marked input normalizer. The
  complete-writer range/cut formula computes a genuine writer source
  when its writer and cut are supplied. At nonzero mark, a composition
  conditional on writer statuses needs the same charged factory schedule
  or a direct scalar-normalizer proof on that input. The nonscalar case
  is kept explicitly. The Bell-corner example is a logical-block example,
  not an identification with the full paid factory-engine cut.

The original full128 preparation source, actual moving-input admission,
