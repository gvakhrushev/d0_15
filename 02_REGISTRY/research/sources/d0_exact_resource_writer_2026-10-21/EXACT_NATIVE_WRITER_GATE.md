### A source budget for an approximate continuation

For the fixed processor at a fixed mesh, let N be its **total** consumed
resource-copy count, including all retry slots, all local trace-copy
multiplicities and the global permutation banks. If each independent
complete resource differs in trace norm by at most epsilon, telescoping
products give a total input error at most N epsilon. Every fixed downstream
instrument contracts this error. Thus a probability secant at field step
delta can have an error of order N epsilon/delta, and recovering a source
scaled by the actual positive gain Gamma requires the corresponding
quantity divided by Gamma to vanish. Fidelity of one active copy alone
does not provide this statement.

For the full finite CQ output, use a continuity bound for the actual
retained output, rather than just the active report. For example, if its

does not cover new dark outcomes or mixed conditional work; those require
their own actual CQ continuity control. One must make the **whole**
downstream error o(Gamma delta), retain the
writer's own failure/work records, and control the **upstream total-price
secant** to the required source. Equal gate length cancels only charges
proved equal on the two actual complete executions. It does not cancel
