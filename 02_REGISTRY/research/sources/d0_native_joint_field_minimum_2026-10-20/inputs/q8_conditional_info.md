## 4. Full finite stopped experiment and quantum oldRIGHT

Let X be a classical hypothesis register and allow any fixed finite cq
state

    rho_XR=(1-pi)|+><+| tensor rho_R^+
           +pi |-><-| tensor rho_R^-,  0<pi<=1.       (6)

rho_R^+ nor rho_R^- is assumed pure, commuting, or distinguishable. The
scalar first-jet apparatus does not act on R. The hypothesis ensemble is
fixed task data; varying pi or these states would add genuine sources.

Run the same actually implemented comparison with fresh work until the
first registered bright, or until N registered dark outcomes. Retain every
failure and the explicit final no-bright outcome. Under +, the latter word
has probability one. Under -, for j=1,...,N,

    P(Y=j|-)=q s^(j-1),  P(Y=infty|-)=s^N,
    s=1-q,  beta=1-s^N,
    E_N(s)=sum_(j=0)^(N-1) s^j.                      (7)

Let B=1_(Y!=infty). Conditional on B=1 the sign is known to be minus, so
the success time carries no further information about X, even with quantum
R. Conditional on B=0 the complete dark word is fixed. The chain rule and
this explicit cq product structure give

    I_phi(X:Y|R)=I_phi(X:B|R)=:I_N(q).              (8)

This is sufficiency for this particular hypothesis objective. The actual
clock Y is still retained and paid; (8) does not identify its full future
price with that of the binary flag.

For completeness let A contain ALL fresh retained computational work,
reference, archive and clock labels beyond the registered word Y. In the
supplied clean implementation (4), each positive-probability terminal word
has, to first order, the same held logical embedding and a fresh-work state
omega_A^Y independent of X conditional on Y. The scalar branch amplitude
contains the entire X and field dependence to this order. The actual
prefix clock is a known field-independent state for each executed length;
it is not reversed when the computational inverse is executed. Thus the
ideal complete cq state has the form

    sum_(x,Y) P(x,Y) |x><x| tensor rho_R^x
               tensor |Y><Y| tensor omega_A^Y.

Its conditional mutual information is exactly I(X:YA|R)=I(X:Y|R).
For a fixed finite N the actual complete state and its first derivative
agree with this ideal state. Under the inherited smooth finite-query
interface their trace-norm difference is O(||dy||^2), including the newly
born plus-bright outcomes and the O(||dy||^4) cleanup-tail probability.
Every marginal has the same estimate. The finite-dimensional entropy
continuity bound O(epsilon |log epsilon|), applied with
epsilon=O(||dy||^2), proves

    I_actual(X:YA|R) = I_N(q(y)) + o(||dy||).

One can obtain that elementary bound by ordering the eigenvalues, bounding
each eigenvalue difference by the trace-norm difference, and integrating

source calculation below applies to the COMPLETE newly retained work,
not only to a preferred pointer quotient. The held logical input is not

For arbitrary fixed quantum R, I_N(q) is nondecreasing in q. To see this,
if beta2>=beta1, obtain B1 from B2 by keeping a bright with probability
beta1/beta2 and otherwise replacing it by dark. This channel fixes the
plus law and changes the minus success beta2 into beta1; it acts on B only.

    I(X:B2|R)-I(X:B1|R)=I(X:B2|R,B1)>=0.            (9)

The last inequality is quantum strong subadditivity. At interior q the

       log_phi[(1-pi+pi s^N)/(pi s^N)] >=0.          (10)

## 6. A genuine accuracy task, finite stopping, and the exact minimum scope

The mandatory Q8 objective can be a real hypothesis-discrimination question,
not an indefinitely pursued bright event. For example require a negative
sheet false-negative probability <=epsilon, where 0<epsilon<p^6, and a
positive sheet false-positive tolerance delta>0, with delta+epsilon<1.
For this concrete task-forcing example take 0<pi<1 and R independent of X.
An X-independent randomized decision which outputs minus with probability
r has errors r and 1-r, and therefore cannot meet these two budgets.
For correlated quantum R a sufficient stronger guard is

    (1-pi) delta + pi epsilon < P_err^*(X|R),
    P_err^*(X|R) = [1-||(1-pi)rho_R^+ - pi rho_R^-||_1]/2.

Then no R-only measurement/decision can meet both budgets. This guard
follows directly by writing its minus-decision effect as 0<=E<=I: the
Bayes error is pi+Tr E[(1-pi)rho_R^+-pi rho_R^-], whose minimum selects
the negative spectral subspace and equals the displayed trace-norm value.
This guard
concerns whether a new physical comparison is compulsory; the source
formula (14) remains valid for every fixed cq R in (6). In particular its
pi=1 or perfectly informative R cases cannot by themselves force another
experiment. At the native point the
above complete rule decides minus on bright, plus after N darks. Its errors
are respectively m^N and zero. The smallest deterministic cutoff is

    N_epsilon = ceil(log(epsilon)/log(m)) >=2.       (15)

If m^N<epsilon<m^(N-1), the selected integer is locally constant. The
strictly positive false-positive budget is slack at the native point since
the perturbed plus bright probability is O(||dy||^2). An exact zero
false-positive constraint off stratum would require separate analysis and
cannot silently support unrestricted field variations. At threshold ties
epsilon=m^N the optimized integer can jump, so a smooth source envelope is
not asserted.

For the class which repeats this SAME instrument and otherwise only stops,
deterministic policies have a single all-dark branch. The first bright
resolves X, so paying another trial after it cannot improve the tested
information. On the all-dark branch every additional trial costs at least
T+1>=2, while its conditional gain about X is at most log_phi 2<2. Hence
every unnecessary continuation strictly increases complete Cost-minus-Info.
Among deterministic cutoff implementations satisfying (15), the true minimum
uses exactly N_epsilon. The same conclusion holds for an actual pathwise
posterior-confidence rule: independent random coins cannot justify an
earlier terminal record whose posterior still fails that rule.

This is NOT an optimum over all native controllers. Under an average-error
constraint, random mixtures of different cutoffs may be admissible and must
be compared. Other allowed experiments may have better cost or information.
The native action/preparation constraints decide that larger domain.

If only the known sign is to be discriminated, (1) already supplies a
working comparison without the added full-J2 query (4). Its error statistics
at the primary point agree with the composed programme and its field source
need not agree. Thus the Q8 accuracy objective by itself does not exclude
that cheaper geometric-source-free implementation. This is the concrete
remaining obstruction to claiming that (14) is physically selected.
