## 2. An exact conditional-noise defect, independent of the oldRIGHT spectrum

Put s=1-q, h=h_phi, and E_N(s)=sum_{j=0}^{N-1}s^j. Under the minus sign,
Y=j has probability q s^(j-1), 1<=j<=N, and Y=infinity has probability
s^N. Under the plus sign, Y=infinity deterministically.

For the ideal complete cq state, the conditional entropy identity gives

    H_phi(YA|R)-I_phi(X:YA|R)
      =H_phi(YA|XR)
      =pi h_phi(q) E_N(1-q).                         (1)

Indeed R is a tensor factor conditional on X, and conditioned fresh work
A is pure. The entropy of the minus stopped word is the sum of the
per-trial Bernoulli entropies weighted by the probability of reaching that
trial: h_phi(q) E_N(1-q). No diagonalization, commutativity, purity or
full-rank hypothesis on rho_R^+ or rho_R^- is used. By the inherited
complete-state estimate, (1) holds to first order for the actual experiment.

Writing K_N(q)=h_phi(q)E_N(1-q),

    K_N'(q)=h_phi'(q)E_N(1-q)-h_phi(q)E_N'(1-q).

    h_phi'(q*)=log_phi(m/q*)=-3-log_phi(4)<0,
