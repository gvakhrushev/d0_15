\[
2\cos(2\theta)=2(p-p^2)=2p^3=2\sqrt5-4.
\tag{10}
\]

every algebraic conjugate of its sum with its inverse would lie in

theta/pi is irrational. In particular sin(2k theta) is nonzero for
every positive integer k, and these rotations are dense on the circle.

\[
0<|\alpha|=2\pi\|k\theta/\pi\|_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}
\le {2\pi\over N+1}.
\tag{11}
\]

epsilon with k<=ceil(2pi/epsilon), hence at most
