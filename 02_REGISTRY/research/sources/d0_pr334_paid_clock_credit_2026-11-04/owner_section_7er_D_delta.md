### 7er.D. What a finite record-entropy interpretation would imply

One important CONDITIONAL application is explicit. Suppose the chosen
Info is a net conditional entropy reduction/telescoping information gain
on an actual fully retained record algebra of dimension M_m, with

    I_m ≤ log_φ M_m + O(1),

and additional work gives no linear negative rebate, −W_m=o(m).
If M_m is polynomial in m, then limsup K_m/m≤0. This one-sided bound
already gives ER5–ER6's obstruction by the same telescoping proof, so it
excludes the claimed full price/clock-source identification. It does not
imply K_m=o(m) unless a matching lower bound is supplied: signed Info
could be very negative. No such lower bound is needed here.

The entropy inequality holds for the stated finite-dimensional entropy
reading (entropy lies between0 and log_φ M); it is NOT inferred for the
unspecified Book Info from the word “information.” A raw POVM label count
does not establish a Hilbert-space capacity bound. Every actual record,
environment, external observer and controller that carries the asserted
gain must be included in M_m. An exponentially large unpriced record
would violate the proposed same-carrier binding, not the entropy bound.

For example, if one claims that all such information is carried by the
very 425 D_m modes priced by the master, take M_m=425D_m. Then it is
O(log m), far below ER5's required linear credit. More generally a
bounded-below W and a valid entropy bound force, from ER5,

    log_φ M_m ≥ 2m−O(log(m+1))−e_m.                     (ER7)

Thus an actual entropy-based repair needs exponentially many retained
distinguishable record states, or a computed linear work rebate, or a
different proved pricing map. Nothing here forbids such a physical
construction; its preparation, source and whole price have to be present.
§7eq prevents supplying those records by an input-sensitive but invisible
refinement of the same complete coherent task. A genuine monitored history
may differ and must retain its changed operators and price.

### 7er.D.1. Consume the owned Golden–Markov rate without assigning its price

[Book06's Iter25 internal Markov realization](../../01_BOOKS/BOOK_06_EVOLUTION_FORGETTING_AND_TIME.md) supplies a positive input,
not a reason to infer that every native clock has sublinear record
entropy. In its stationary Parry law, with p=φ^(-1) and p+p²=1,

    P = [[p,p²],[1,0]],       π = (1,p²)/(1+p²).

Indeed πP=π. The conditional entropy of row0 in φ units is p+2p²=1+p²;
row1 has entropy0. Their π-weighted mean is exactly1. The Markov chain
rule therefore gives, for a genuinely registered stationary prefix of
n≥1 symbols,

    H_φ(R_0,...,R_(n−1)) = H_φ(π)+(n−1).                (ER7a)

Thus n=2m+O(1) has the linear scale required by ER5. Such a retained
history also has exponentially many distinguishable words; it is not
subject to the polynomial-record hypothesis of §7er.D. These statements
use the already owned symbolic/Parry law, not a new toral conjugacy or
an assumption about the full growing-clock operator's periodicity.
The book and [AdlerWeissInternalMarkov.lean](../../03_FORMALIZATION/D0/VNext2/AdlerWeissInternalMarkov.lean) retain the separate metric
embedding/measure-transport boundary.

The required application is an actual stage/record/preparation map.
H.1's canonical births and CX padding do not register a Parry symbol at
every paid stage merely because both constructions involve φ. Their
complete retained output, initial ensemble, clocks and prices still
need a common map. Shannon entropy is not thereby selected as Book Info.
Its conditioning on the actual initial knowledge/seed must also agree;
marginal randomness alone does not establish new retained information.
In particular, a fixed field-independent Parry law has zero field
derivative of ER7a. Under §7eq's exact faithful extension of ONE coherent
H.1 catalogue, the record is also independent of that catalogue's SAM
label, so its large entropy is not acquired information about that label.
This reuses EQ1–EQ4, including all coherent cross tests.

The quantifiers matter: at separate executions V(Y), EQ1 permits
c_(ω,j)(Y); their moving record probabilities, actual branch prices,
work and preparation derivatives survive. No field neutrality is claimed
for those variables or for the full coherent operator-entropy source.
Consequently ER7a can supply a candidate duration-credit scale, but
neither an independent Einstein field covector nor its physical price
binding. It removes no B–D premise without that missing application.

There is a concrete whole-price consequence of retaining this candidate.
All binary words of length n without 11 have positive Parry probability;
their number is F_(n+2), with F_1=F_2=1. If they are mutually distinguishable
terminal records, the actual final fibre has dimension at least F_(n+2).
Already n=10 gives144 histories, exceeding the six-wire probe's entire
64-dimensional terminal fibre; using its larger temporal direct sum as

For an extension satisfying the SAME growing-clock and complete RAW

therefore obey

    D'_m ≥ F_(n+2),   H'_m ≥ β^(-1) log[359 F_(n+2)].   (ER7b)

Here D'=Σ_j dim H'_j includes the actual terminal record stage. The
Fibonacci recurrence gives log F_(n+2)=n logφ+O(1); with n=2m+O(1),
liminf H'_m/m≥2logφ/β. Thus the retained prefix cannot simply be inserted
as linear credit while keeping the original six-wire O(log m) heat.
Its additional carrier changes the priced clock itself. ER7b is a
conditional bound for this actual enlarged construction, not a claim
that H.1 has implemented it, or a no-go for other proved complete-price
transports. Nonorthogonal outputs, an erased/reconstructed prefix or an
external register do not meet the stated retained-record hypothesis;
each would need its own actual information, resource and price map.
Only heat is bounded here. The extended feedback J', primitive costs,
Info/work and their joint sources must be computed; ER1 does not prove
that appending this record preserves J_0 or the original return gain.
