## 2. A unique metric from a kinetic density, without a metric fit

contravariant tensor density of weight +1 in four dimensions, with fixed

    K^{mu nu}=sqrt(-det G) G^{mu nu}.

It is

    G=sqrt(-det K) K^{-1}.                                  (1)

**Proof.** Taking determinants in K=sqrt(-det G)G^{-1} gives
`det K=det G` in dimension four. The positive volume factor is therefore
`sqrt(-det K)`, and inversion gives (1). Conversely (1) reconstructs K by
direct multiplication and determinant calculation. No conformal factor is
left free. In dimension two this uniqueness fails; the checker includes
that negative control. A fixed overall material-field normalization is
needed: it is the existing CAR scalar normalization, not a new SI anchor.

For any symmetric variation delta K, including off-diagonal directions,

    delta G=sqrt(-det K)[
       (1/2)tr(K^{-1}delta K)K^{-1}-K^{-1}delta K K^{-1}].     (2)

Thus the dictionary has a full source derivative, not only a value map.

## 7. What is sufficient for exact roots, with the missing bound exposed

Here is a complete quantitative theorem that actually yields roots rather
than just small source errors. It allows saddle Hessians.

Let F_h=D Jcal_h be the COMPLETE reduced field equation on a genuine
constraint/gauge-resolved finite chart. Let S_h^0 be a reference functional
with a known stationary point y0 and invertible A_h=D^2 S_h^0(y0). Assume
on a closed ball around y0:

    ||A_h^{-1}||<=K_h,
    ||F_h(y0)||<=eta_h,
    ||D F_h(y)-D^2 S_h^0(y)||<=delta_h,
    ||D^2 S_h^0(y)-A_h||<=L_h ||y-y0||.

Set r_h=2K_h eta_h. If that ball lies in the admitted chart and

    K_h(delta_h+2L_h K_h eta_h)<=1/2,                        (14)

then F_h has a unique zero in the ball, within r_h of y0.

**Proof.** Use T(y)=y-A_h^{-1}F_h(y). Its derivative has norm at most the
left side of (14), and ||T(y0)-y0||<=K_h eta_h=r_h/2. Thus T maps the ball
into itself and contracts by at most 1/2. The fixed point solves F_h=0.
Completeness of the finite-dimensional closed ball gives existence and
uniqueness. If eta_h=0 the supplied centre is already a root; a separate
positive-radius derivative bound gives local uniqueness. No field minimum
or positive-definite Hessian is assumed.

This is a sufficient recovery route, not an added universal axiom. Other
stable or topological recovery methods may work. The C2 estimate (13) can
supply eta_h and delta_h once its norm is the theorem's norm; it does NOT
supply K_h, an admitted gauge slice, or a mesh-uniform boundary problem.
The current proof has not computed those data for the general D0 lattice.

Why that cannot be skipped: S_h(x)=x^3/3+h x converges smoothly to S_0=x^3/3
on every compact interval, yet S_h'(x)=x^2+h has no real zero when h>0,
while S_0'(0)=0. Thus even C2 consistency alone cannot logically assert
root recovery at a degenerate limit. The example is a mathematical control,
not a counterexample to D0 or GR.
