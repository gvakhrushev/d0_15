For the 128 fixed original histories saved in the adjacent selector report, the pinned original constructor and `effect_of_word` produced the 128×128 even/odd-block coordinate matrix. No alternative histories were searched. Odd slots were normalized by the inverse of 626 per occurrence, corresponding to the already proved positive odd-port factor. The raw determinant is 879, and the normalized determinant is 219 modulo 1009. `certificate.json` records elementary elimination data; `tools/verify_fixed_witness.py` independently checked those operations and recomputed both determinants with a separate pivot strategy. The generator and checker were independently read before the run; the selector module is trusted executable code.

`metadata.json` pins input, generator and guard hashes, output hashes, selected prime and resource observation. One fixed-witness run and one checker run completed successfully. Their output contains no personal paths. The local `tools/extract_original_future_witness.py` is the pinned guard used by the generator; its hash is recorded.

This is a finite calculation in one field, not a proof of the eight-prime historical run or complex linear independence. The equality of this matrix with the images of the actual local reduced Lean effects, including the original interpolation, conjugation and chronological order, is still an open application. The generic determinant theorem cannot discharge that premise by itself. The complete physical execution, owner #334, and `GR_closed=false` remain unchanged.
