## 7. Sharp obstruction to additive positive-pointer assembly

For every finite cap `N>=1`, the total probability of newly born pointer leaves is

```

```

```

 = smooth_function(t) + (p/ell)t^2 ln(t^2)

```

The remainder can include higher analytic terms; the displayed leading singular coefficient is positive and cap-independent.

Consider finitely many such scalar reporters with positive multiplicities `n_j`, all their leaf records retained, additive actual code costs, and complete joint pointer information. If their leaf effects are scalar, conditional probabilities of subsequent reporters are input-independent even with previous work retained as a reference. The joint distribution is the product of the individual distributions, so complete pointer entropy is additive. More generally the following statement applies whenever this actual product structure has been proved.

At a common-null point with `t_j=0`, suppose the full first variation, including a `C^2` actual remainder `W`, cancels. Along a smooth field curve `y(epsilon)` with `a_j=dt_j[y'(0)]`,

```

 = (p/ell) [sum_j n_j a_j^2] epsilon^2 ln(epsilon^2)
   + O(epsilon^2).
```

If at least one `a_j !=0`, the leading term is strictly negative for both signs of sufficiently small nonzero epsilon. The point is not a local minimum. Geometric and matter first sources can cancel linearly; their positive entropy-rank-birth coefficients cannot cancel.
