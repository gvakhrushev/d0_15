## A concrete exclusion: cleanup debt cannot supply the missing advantage

Suppose the proposed continuation merely completes or repairs the actual
non-clean work and its fixed finite price depends on clean versus dirty
work, not on an independently meaningful reporter bit. G^k acts on the
port and preserves the complete clean/dirty work probabilities. Its
expected future price has the form

    V_k(t) = (1-r(t)) v_clean + r(t) v_dirty

in the two-price case; the same conclusion at Sigma holds for any bounded
work-only price table. Therefore

    V_k|Sigma = v_clean for every k,
    DV_k|Sigma = 0.

There is neither a k=2 selection advantage on Sigma nor an extra first
source. The complete cleanup theorem already forces this, since all dirty
probabilities are O(t^4). Calling bit zero "failure" cannot alter that fact:
it relabels the rotated port rather than the actual non-clean work.

Likewise a common required continuation adds the same V to every candidate
and leaves the proved terminal-price rejection unchanged. To use the
conditional five-or-six-tick theorem, the task must establish a genuinely
bit-dependent difference of full remaining prices, including correlations
and moving-price derivatives. It cannot be obtained from copied deterministic
report/clock records, which add no independent Shannon entropy
(owner lines 408-409).
