## 4. Complete-process inequality, including readiness and abort

There is an elementary proof without assuming a measurement is
classical before the actual report. Let Phi=C^tensor n tensor ancillary
purification, and let K_r be the **complete** instrument maps for the
replacement, including retained quantum work and adaptive outcome
histories. Their matrices do not depend on the varied amplitude, and
sum_r K_r^*K_r=I. For any positive outcome probability
P_r=||K_r Phi||², Cauchy–Schwarz gives

```text
(dot P_r)²/P_r <= 4 ||K_r dot Phi||².
```

Summing and using completeness yields

```text
I_classical = sum_r (dot P_r)²/P_r
             <= 4 ||dot Phi||² = 4n ||dot C||².            (6)
```

Zero-probability sectors may be treated by their limiting nonnegative
contribution; omitting them cannot weaken the necessary bound from
the positive coarse outcomes below. No quantum work or reference is
traced away in deriving completeness.

Coarsen the actual retained record to

```text
ready/no-click: beta d,   ready/click: beta(1-d),
abort: 1-beta.
```

Coarsening cannot increase the displayed classical information: on
each merged set this is another Cauchy–Schwarz inequality. For
0<beta<1 its exact value is

```text
I_coarse = beta (dot d)²/[d(1-d)]
                    + (dot beta)²/[beta(1-beta)].          (7)
```

Thus even field-dependent readiness cannot remove the required first
term. The declared Golden bank/detector has dot beta=0; extra retained
retry labels cannot reduce this necessary information. Beta=1 has
the same first term directly, with no abort sector.

Insert (2), (4) and (5) into I_coarse<=I_classical in (6).
The factors four cancel, giving exactly (1).

## 6. What supplies the missing information, and what remains open

The theorem grants arbitrary fixed gates and effects, so replacing
Golden logic by a larger fixed palette cannot avoid it. It also grants
the full128 purification, which is at least as informative as DATA
alone. It does not grant a psi-dependent controller, amplitude code,
state-preparation oracle, supplied moving b(q) gate, or a factory archive
carrying additional material information.

For a general complete pure input Xi(psi), the same proof instead gives
the bound 4||D Xi||² (or its phase-orthogonal version). Additional
material-dependent archives and coherent programme registers must be
included there. A variable gate contributes its own derivative to
D(K_r Xi), so the fixed-K_r proof does not apply without that term.
These are precisely candidate **physical input maps** to construct
and price, not counterexamples to (1).

Similarly, local tensor-product CAR access, repeated regeneration,
different correlations and a variable number of consumed copies need
their own declared input/clock model. This result bounds n copies in
the specified complete initial resource. It does not turn a maximum
copy cap into an expected execution cost or assign an operational tick
to physical continuum time.

There is no contradiction with the exact Golden detector's finite
128N resource bank: those resources are field independent, and that
theorem explicitly supplies the moving query as an input. The present
bound concerns an attempted replacement of that supplied input.

The parent route now requires an explicit material-access mechanism
meeting this information budget, rather than identifying unknown
amplitudes with a classical CODE Dpsi. The positive local-query
construction, compulsory task/Book03 price, every independent
preparation source, complete H/R terms and physical GR closure remain
open. A different programme preserving only the summed physical
equations is not excluded by this local-query result.
