The original complete return effect obeys

    J2=-Tr log(I-zF),                 Pi*=z(I-zF*)^-1,

    t=Tr[C(F-F*)]/(2N_h),             gamma_h=Q0R0/(2N_h).

directions v,w,

    H_t[v,w]=gamma_h Tr(Pi* D²F[v,w]),
    H_J2[v,w]=Tr(Pi* D²F[v,w])
              +Tr(Pi* DF[v] Pi* DF[w]),
    H_t=gamma_h(H_J2-Q_F).                              (4)

Pi*>0 on the varying support and DF is Hermitian. It need not vanish even
at DJ2=0. Thus H_t=gamma_h H_J2 cannot be assumed. The proof below computes
the actual anchor and automatically includes this distinction.
