## 2. Raw normalized-record binding: source exists, but the programme minimum does not

Suppose the additional absolute Book binding is w_k(n)=P_k(n). The owned
pathwise cocycle then forces

    Info_raw(n)=log_phi(P_k(n)/mu_k(n)),
    S_raw(n)=C_k(n)-Info_raw(n)=-log_phi P_k(n),
    E S_raw=H_geo(q):=h_phi(q)/q.                      (3)

The relative information is relative to the subprobability mu. In normalized
notation,

    E Info_raw=D_phi(P_k || mu_k/Z_k)-log_phi Z_k.

It would be wrong to call the first expression ordinary KL against a
normalized distribution, or to omit its -log Z term.
Equation (3) exactly cancels all actually paid costs from the NET price by
crediting them in Info; it does not make execution free. That credit is an
extra consequence of w=P, not a consequence of complete record retention.

For the inherited full signal t, at Sigma q'_k=4 cos(k theta)sin(k theta).
The exact fixed-programme field source is

    H_geo'(q)=log_phi(1-q)/q²,
    D E S_raw=log_phi(1-q_k) q'_k/q_k² Dt.             (4)

The coefficient is nonzero for every finite k>=1. At k=1 it is
-4/p^(5/2), and at k=2 it is -3/p^(3/2). Fine fresh-work entropy is
O(t^4|log|t||) for each fixed k and has zero first jet, so (4) is also the
full tape first source under this binding. This statement is not an exact

However theta/pi is irrational, so k theta modulo pi is dense. There is a
sequence q_k->1, while no finite k has q_k=1. Since H_geo(q)>0 for 0<q<1
and H_geo(q)->0 as q->1,

    inf_(k>=1) E S_raw=0, and no finite k attains it.   (5)

Moreover E C_k>=B+T+k ell+2 diverges along such a sequence, and E Info_raw
also diverges. This theorem concerns the SAME unbounded k-family used by

As successive cutoffs exhaust the k-family, minimizing indices escape

## 3. Normalize the cost reference while retaining the actual costs

Now suppose instead Info_norm(n)=log_phi(P_k(n)/(mu_k(n)/Z_k)). This is
a different absolute binding, even though the same actual Born law is used.
Keeping (1), the exact action becomes

    S_norm(n)=-log_phi P_k(n)-log_phi Z_k,
    w_k(n)=Z_k P_k(n),
    E S_norm=H_geo(q_k)-log_phi Z_k.                  (6)

Thus normalized cost-reference KL identifies the otherwise unknown absolute
Book weight with the *cost partition* Z_k. This is an explicit candidate
binding, not a proof that native Book chooses it. The correct inference is
that changing this normalization changes the absolute weight; one cannot
renormalize mu and silently keep w=P.

Write

    b(A)=A+2+log_phi(1-p^(A+1)).

Then E S_norm=B+b(T+k ell)+H_geo(q_k), b'(A)=1/(1-p^(A+1))>1,
and b(A)->infinity. This objective is coercive in k, so a finite minimum
exists for every T,ell>0. Yet Z_k is field-independent under the stipulated

nonattaining raw binding. Fixed-programme sources alone therefore do not

    E Cost-I_phi(X:tape|R)>=B+T+k ell+2-H_phi(X|R).    (8)
