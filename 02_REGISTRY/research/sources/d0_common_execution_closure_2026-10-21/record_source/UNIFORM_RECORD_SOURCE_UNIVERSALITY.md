## 2. One binary report: two spectral strata, all complete labels retained

Fix one successful logical-factory transcript. Let `t=1,...,M` be the COMPLETE address/Bell transcript. Write

\[
q_{t1}=p_t r_t,\qquad q_{t0}=p_t(1-r_t),\qquad\sum_t p_t=1.
\tag{1}
\]

At the reference point `y0`, suppose

\[
p_t(y_0)=1/M,\qquad r_t(y_0)=r_0\in(0,1).
\tag{2}
\]

The second equality is the physically implemented matched-abort condition: failed resource banks emit a genuinely supplied reference report of probability `r0`; all successful primary reports also have probability `r0`. No failure record is discarded.

Let the actual conditional pure work be `|u_tb(y)>`. The complete CQ state is

\[
\Gamma(y)=\bigoplus_{t,b}q_{tb}(y)|u_{tb}(y)\rangle\langle u_{tb}(y)|.
\tag{3}
\]

Its positive eigenvalues are exactly the complete probabilities `q_tb`. The remaining eigenvalues stay zero on the stated pure-conditional-work manifold. A changing work vector, including a changing retained reference, changes eigenvectors but not this spectrum.

Consider a differentiable spectral valuation

\[
\mathcal V(\Gamma)=f(q_{10},q_{11},\ldots,q_{M0},q_{M1}),
\tag{4}
\]

where `f` is a symmetric `C1` function on the positive probability simplex. Differentiability is required on the displayed fixed-rank family; no derivative of Shannon entropy at a newly born eigenvalue is presumed.

**Theorem 1.** At (2), there is one scalar `c_f` such that, for EVERY admitted variation,

\[
D\mathcal V=c_f\,D\mathcal R,
\qquad
\mathcal R=\sum_t q_{t1}=\sum_t p_t r_t.
\tag{5}
\]

If `a_b` denotes the partial derivative of `f` at any coordinate in report stratum `b`, then `c_f=a_1-a_0`.

**Proof.** Symmetry forces the differential of `f` to be constant on each set of equal positive probabilities. Under (2), these sets include all `M` coordinates with the same report bit. Thus

\[
D\mathcal V
=a_1\sum_t Dq_{t1}+a_0\sum_t Dq_{t0}
=(a_1-a_0)D\mathcal R.
\]

Adding a common constant to the simplex gradient has no effect. Also

\[
D\mathcal R
=r_0\sum_t Dp_t+M^{-1}\sum_t Dr_t
=M^{-1}\sum_t Dr_t.
\tag{6}
\]

Hence every normal change of the complete label probabilities cancels from the first source. The work-vector variations contribute zero by (3)-(4). This proves (5) without fixing the label probabilities off the reference point. If `r0=1/2`, full spectral symmetry forces `c_f=0`; nonzero response is an additional necessary condition. QED.

For the actual processing price `constant - H_phi(Gamma)`,

\[
c_f=\log_\varphi\frac{r_0}{1-r_0}=-h_\varphi'(r_0).
\tag{7}
\]

It is nonzero on the owned primary report, where `0<r0<1/2`. As other mathematical examples, `Tr Gamma^k` has

\[
c_f=kM^{1-k}\big[r_0^{k-1}-(1-r_0)^{k-1}\big],\qquad k\ge2.
\tag{8}
\]

These examples illustrate source equivalence; they are not candidate physical replacements for Info. The trace itself has `c_f=0` and supplies no gravitational source.

If all successful resource-bank transcripts have the same differentiable conditional report `r_sharp(y)` on the diagonal resource section, and failure reports are fixed at `r0`, let `s0` be the fraction of successful transcripts at the reference point. Then (6) becomes

\[
D\mathcal V=c_f s_0\,Dr_\sharp.
\tag{9}
\]

Away from the diagonal resource section different transcripts can have different report derivatives; (5)-(6), not (9), are the general statement. A measured transitive resource permutation supplies their correct first-order averaging.

## 3. Factory flags and an actual measured permutation

Logical-factory transcripts have probabilities independent of the admitted data and resources. On a factory abort, retain all work coherently and make no data-dependent readout; the conditional state is pure. On a successful transcript, (1)-(3) apply. Such a factory abort contributes only its fixed eigenvalue to a spectral valuation.

For a general spectral function of the FULL CQ state, the derivatives `a_b` can depend on the successful factory transcript because its probability rescales the conditional eigenvalues. One must retain the resulting weighted sum of coefficients. Equality of all sources across spatial cells follows if their COMPLETE factory profiles and schedules are identical, not merely if their scalar success probabilities coincide. A common finite catalogue of all required constant preparations can be padded and executed at every local cell; this is extra paid work. Alternatively the actual valuation may have a proved additive conditional chain rule, as Shannon entropy does. No chain rule is inferred for a general spectral function.

Similarly, an actual measured uniform permutation label `pi` produces a direct sum of conditional outputs. For Shannon entropy the full price equals a constant plus the arithmetic mean of the conditional prices. For a general spectral function this equality need NOT hold off the diagonal.

At a diagonal resource state, however, all permutation-conditioned probability vectors coincide, up to record relabelling. The differential of a symmetric spectral function is identical in all these repeated blocks. Therefore its first source is the average of the conditional first sources for the corresponding repeated-spectrum valuation. This is the first-order statement needed for transitive independent-copy cancellation. It must not be promoted to equality of the complete off-diagonal prices or their Hessians.

## 4. The complete spatial ledger is a product, not a binary report

The native ledger has literal independent local report copies, with integer multiplicities `m_z=nu*528*D_j`. Keep them as tensor factors; never replace the sum by the entropy of a spatially averaged report.

Let there be `L=sum_z m_z` factors. Conditioned on the actual permutation and factory records, their classical probability vectors are `q_1,...,q_L`. For a symmetric differentiable spectral function of the entire product CQ state, define

\[
F_L(q_1,\ldots,q_L)=F(q_1\otimes\cdots\otimes q_L).
\tag{10}
\]

At the common primary point every factor has the same complete profile `q_*`, with the uniform-label / matched-report property in each successful-factory slice.

In (11), `R_i` is the common conditional report probability of cell `i` on a successful logical-factory slice, including its resource-bank successes and matched aborts. The logical-factory weights are included in `c_L`; a factory abort has no data-dependent report and contributes no derivative. This convention avoids identifying that conditional probability with an unconditioned record indicator.

**Theorem 2.** Under these actual product and identical-profile hypotheses,

\[
DF_L=c_L\sum_{i=1}^L D\mathcal R_i
\tag{11}
\]

for one scalar `c_L` when all variable successful slices have the same conditional report response. Equivalently, with a common physical readiness `s0` and diagonal resource reports `r_sharp,z`,

\[
DF_L=c_Ls_0\sum_zm_z Dr_{\sharp,z}.
\tag{12}
\]

Here the definition of `c_L` includes the actual common factory-profile weights. It can vanish and must be checked for the actual valuation.

**Proof.** Differentiate one product factor in (10). The resulting marginal differential is a symmetric function of that factor's complete labels. At `q_i=q_*`, all factor marginals are the same, because permuting tensor factors permutes the full eigenvalue list. Within a successful-factory slice the marginal gradient is constant on the two report strata. Apply the local calculation to each slice and factor, retaining the slice weights. The product spectrum generally has MANY eigenvalues (already `L+1` report Hamming-weight strata without factory variation), not just two. The proof uses its permutation symmetry, not a false two-eigenvalue reduction. QED.

The condition in (11) about equal conditional response matters: without it the exact expression is the sum of the separate factory-slice marginal coefficients times their own report responses. In the fixed-processor construction, every successful logical-factory history implements the same ideal circuit and common query-bank schedule; failed factories contribute no resource-dependent readout. This is the stated consumer.

For the Shannon processing price, additivity gives `c_L=-gamma_F h_phi'(r0)` independently of `L`, where `gamma_F` is the actual common logical-factory completion probability in the complete local profile. If one conditions on factory completion before defining the profile, the corresponding conditional coefficient is `-h_phi'(r0)`. The complete compiler source uses the former coefficient. For a nonadditive spectral valuation, `c_L` can depend on the full record dimension and all fixed factory probabilities.

## 6. Sharp controls

1. **No matched abort.** If failure uses `r_abort != r0`, then even the Shannon source contains `Ds [h_phi(r0)-h_phi(r_abort)]`. Uniform success data alone does not remove that source.
2. **Non-spectral record charge.** Add `epsilon*p_1` as a physically unequal label charge. A variation transferring probability between labels changes the price while keeping the total report probability fixed. Equation (5) then fails, exactly because the new charge is outside the hypothesis.
3. **Unequal cells.** For `r_1=r0+t`, `r_2=r0-t`, identical cell valuations have zero collective first source at `t=0`; unequal multipliers generally do not. Equality of the complete coefficient across sites cannot be omitted.
4. **Zero spectral response.** `Tr Gamma=1` is spectral and label symmetric but has zero source. Symmetry alone does not select gravity.

The companion checker verifies (5) and the product argument with exact rational arithmetic for several nonadditive polynomial spectral functions, together with the unequal-label and unequal-cell controls. The analytic proof, rather than those examples, establishes the general statement.
