### 2. The derivative theorem uses the whole spatial bank

At the diagonal, a varied resource visits every original slot exactly
once under its cyclic label. Thus each independent first covector is
the collective diagonal covector divided by the padded bank size and
multiplied by the actual permutation readiness. This does not require
the original local roles or coefficients to be equal.

The bank is the union of all independent local implementations with

material Euler cancellation can occur between different sites and
trace copies. A separate permutation inside each local report would
not in general make that local report stationary. Conditional on the
retained global label, disjoint input factors and local transcript laws
