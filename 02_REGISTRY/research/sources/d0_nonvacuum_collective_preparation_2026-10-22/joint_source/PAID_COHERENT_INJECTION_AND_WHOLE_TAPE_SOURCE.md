## 2. Uniform j-label from the existing paid fair-bit factories

finite-cap H factories. Compute the Boolean predicate `u<j` reversibly on the
coherent label u and measure only that **herald**. A successful active label is

    |J_j> = j^(-1/2) sum_(u=0,...,j-1) |u> |retained comparator work(u)>.

This is an executed postselection with success a_j=j/2^m>1/2. No square-root
gate is supplied. The retained label u itself is not measured. All attempts

clock remain in the complete execution. The success indicator is independent
of the bank because this label is prepared before it interacts with the bank.
For j=1 use the deterministic zero label, with its actual fixed preparation

Conditional on every successful detailed transcript, the selected active

attempt supplies it is a classical transcript-controlled routing operation;
all of its addressed gates are included in the fixed schedule.

## 3. Coherent partial Fisher–Yates, not a rare global herald

selected bank, its correct entire factor must travel with that bank.

The k retained labels are mutually orthogonal records of the choices, kept

    V_(N,k) = [(N)_k]^(-1/2) sum_iota |lambda_iota> P_iota,

where iota ranges over all ordered injections of k slots into N bank positions,
(N)_k=N!/(N−k)!, and P_iota is the actual partial Fisher–Yates permutation.
The labels include all unmeasured comparison scratch, so distinct choices
remain orthogonal. Hence V*V=I. Apply the unchanged k-slot programme to the
first k positions and retain all other positions and selector labels.

The configured reports have no downstream gate whose actual operation requires
an already measured report. Keep **all logical report bits coherent until the
entire finite logical schedule has succeeded**; only then measure the full
report tape and execute its actual original 0/10 emitters. This is a declared
schedule of the changed programme. Its successful joint law agrees with the
old nonadaptive logical programme because subsequent operations act on its

    E_(ω,N) = 1/(N)_k sum_iota P_iota* (E_ω tensor I) P_iota.       (1)

symmetrize E_ω over the k roles and apply the usual subset U-statistic. No
