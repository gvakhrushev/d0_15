## 1. Sector

Fix one of the six physical identity-solder resonance circles and its real
one-envelope invariant carrier.  Write the odd four-phase connection center
by a complex periodic envelope \(Z_n\), \(n=x_0+x_1\); the second real
quadrature is the phase translate of the first.  All even-q connection
variables are allowed.  No additional odd quarter-center or coupled-circle
coordinate is included in this theorem.

Let \(F_{\rm fast}\) denote the connection Euler rows together with the
noncommon four-phase metric rows.  A smooth prescribed source enters only the
metric slots; for the homogeneous flat source \(F_{\rm fast}=0\).

The result below is uniform in the envelope period and hence in lattice
refinement.

## 2. Exact inputs

### Uniform even solve

Both even connection blocks generated by an arbitrary circle envelope have
exact finite Laurent inverses.  Their coefficient row and column envelopes
are \(9/2\).  Hence on every \(\ell^p\), including \(p=\infty\) and the raw
owner \(p=1\),

\[
\|H_{\rm even}^{-1}\|_{p\to p}\le\frac92
\tag{1}
\]

independently of the period.

Finite-stencil analyticity therefore gives a period-independent contraction
for the even variables.  If

\[
t=\|Z\|_\infty
\]

and the full link chart is sufficiently small, the exact even connection
equations determine

\[
e=\Gamma_{\rm even}(Z),\qquad
\|e\|_\infty\le C t^2,
\tag{2}
\]

with \(C\) independent of the period.  With an even connection residual
\(s\), the right side becomes \(C(t^2+\|s\|_\infty)\).

### Complete quadratic metric gate

After the exact even quadratic correction, the alternating metric coefficient
is the owned local expression

\[
Q_2(Z)_n=
2\operatorname{Re}\left(
iZ_n^2+P_n,-2P_n,
\frac i2(Z_{n+1}^2-Z_n^2),
\frac i2(Z_{n+1}^2-Z_n^2),
-iZ_n^2+P_n,
-\frac i2(Z_{n+1}^2-Z_n^2),
-\frac i2(Z_{n+1}^2-Z_n^2),0,0,0
\right),
\tag{3}
\]

\[
P_n=Z_{n+1}^2-Z_{n+1}Z_{n+2}+Z_{n+2}^2 .
\]

Its exact zero set on a connected nonzero envelope is

\[
Z_n=t\epsilon_n i^n
\quad\text{or}\quad
Z_n=i\,t\epsilon_n i^n,
\qquad
\epsilon_n\in\{\pm1\}.
\tag{4}
\]

A zero propagates, so there is no additional component with isolated zero
sites.

### All-period cubic left witness

On either cone (4), after the unique even quadratic correction, the positive-q
cubic joint source \(s_3\) obeys the coefficientwise Laurent identity

\[
L(T)J(T)=0,\qquad
L(T)s_3=\frac i4\,T\epsilon ,
\tag{5}
\]

where the finite witness has coefficient envelope 36.  Consequently

\[
\|s_3+JV\|_p\ge\frac{t^3}{18}\|\epsilon\|_p
\tag{6}
\]

for every period and every \(1\le p\le\infty\), with arbitrary odd cubic
normal correction \(V\).

## 3. Uniform blow-up theorem

**Theorem.** There are \(\varepsilon>0\) and \(C<\infty\), independent of
the envelope period, such that every field in the sector above with
\(\|\log K\|_\infty<\varepsilon\) and exact connection stationarity satisfies

\[
\boxed{
t^3
\le C\,\|\Pi_{\rm fast}E_Q\|_\infty .
}
\tag{7}
\]

More generally the right side may include the sup norm of the connection
residual if stationarity is approximate.

In particular,

\[
E_K=0,\qquad \Pi_{\rm fast}E_Q=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Z=0
\tag{8}
\]

through one common radius \(\varepsilon\) for **all periods**.

### Proof

Assume (7) is false.  Then there are periods \(L_j\), nonzero fields with
\(t_j\to0\), and fast residuals \(s_j\) such that

\[
\frac{\|s_j\|_\infty}{t_j^3}\to0 .
\tag{9}
\]

Use the uniform even inverse (1) and the analytic finite-stencil equation.
After dividing by \(t_j^2\), the exact fast metric equations give

\[
Q_2(Z^{(j)}/t_j)=o(1)
\tag{10}
\]

uniformly on every fixed stencil window.

Choose \(n_j\) with
\(|Z^{(j)}_{n_j}|=t_j\).  The normalized envelopes are bounded by one.
On a fixed window large enough to contain the supports of (3) and (5), pass
to a subsequence.  Equation (10) makes the limit an exact local zero of the
quadratic gate.  Since the central value has modulus one, the zero-propagation
argument in the exact quadratic classification forces that entire required
window into one of the two sign cones (4), with nonzero common magnitude.

Now divide the positive-q joint equation by \(t_j^3\) and apply the same
finite witness \(L(T)\).  The linear range is killed by (5).  The even
variable converges to its unique quadratic correction by (1)--(2).  Every
remaining analytic term is \(o(1)\): the finite action has a fixed local
stencil and all derivatives are uniformly bounded on the common compact
link chart.  The source term is \(o(1)\) by (9).

The limit is therefore the cubic witness on a sign cone.  Equation (5) makes
it \(\frac i4T\epsilon\), which is nonzero at the chosen finite window.
Contradiction.

Only a fixed stencil window is compactified; no compactness in a
growing-dimensional lattice space and no period-dependent normal-chart
constant is used.  This proves (7).

## 4. Smooth-source consequence

For a fixed \(C^\infty\) source sampled on a refining torus, its projection
to these checkerboard/quarter fast rows is \(O(h^\infty)\) in every fixed
smooth Fourier/Wiener norm; the #216 tail/alias lemma supplies the same
super-algebraic statement after sampling.

Equation (7) then gives

\[
\boxed{t_h=O(h^\infty).}
\tag{11}
\]

Finite-stencil analyticity converts (11) into a super-algebraic difference of
every local metric readout in this pure-circle sector.  The polynomial factor
from the unweighted \(L^4\) site count and the final \(h^{-2}\) normalization
does not destroy \(O(h^\infty)\).

Thus pure physical resonance-circle envelopes cannot create a distinct
smooth-source response class merely by increasing their period.

## 5. What this removes

The previous all-period certificate had a period-independent cubic inequality
but stated only fixed-period tangent isolation because a generic
finite-dimensional normal chart can deteriorate with \(L\).

The present proof does **not** use that chart.  It uses:

1. the explicit \(L\)-uniform even Laurent inverse;
2. the local quadratic envelope formula;
3. one maximum point and a fixed stencil window;
4. the exact all-period cubic left witness.

Therefore the refinement-uniform statement (7) is not an inference from
pointwise finite-dimensional isolation.

In source-image language,

\[
\boxed{
\text{the small pure-circle envelope sector contributes only the designated
flat source class in the smooth-source limit.}
}
\tag{12}
\]
