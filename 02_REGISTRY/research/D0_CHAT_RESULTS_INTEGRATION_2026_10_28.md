## Original Book03 source with a moving active cut

The [upstream moving-cut note](A4D_ROOT_BOOTSTRAP_MOVING_CUT_SOURCE_2026_10_28.md)
and its [proof, original checker and historical outputs](sources/d0_root_moving_cut_2026-10-28/PROOF_RU.md)
are retained at their original repository paths and bytes. For a finite
positive-Hilbert carrier, unitary `U`, orthogonal fixed-rank `P`, Hermitian
`Delta`, fixed `beta > 0`, `0 < z < 1` and an actual differentiable admitted
variation, define `Q=I-P`, `C=QUP`, `F=C* C`, `R=(I-zF)^(-1)`,
`rho=exp(-beta Delta)/Tr exp(-beta Delta)` and `K=[dP,P]`. The exact source of
the stated Book03 action is

```text
nabla U = dU - [K,U],       nabla Delta = dDelta - [K,Delta]
dB = -Tr(rho nabla Delta) + 2 z Re Tr(R C* Q(nabla U)P).
```

This retains `dP`: common internal co-transport has zero source, while relative
cut/dynamics motion can contribute. If β, z, the inner-product Gram, rank or
additional physical costs vary, their actual terms or separate treatment must
be retained. The displayed formula itself neither proves the differentiable
native field family nor supplies the map from its actual field variations to
`(dU,dP,dDelta)`.

The original finite checker was rerun in normal and optimized modes. A
separate [Frechet-derivative control](sources/d0_chat_integration_checks_2026-10-28/README.md)
checks finite complex matrices, ranks including 0 and full rank, separate β/z
variations, co-transport and noncommuting matrices. Its inputs and tolerances
are recorded; it is numerical evidence under the stated conditions, not a
Lean theorem, a full42 replay or proof that the source is Einstein. The
original Golden splitter uses the same signs as Book03; the identity
`F=p²P` is a finite control and does not establish that its parameter is an
independently admissible variation.

## What was actually replayed

All 12 distinct Python checkers in the nine packages were run from isolated
copies in normal and optimized modes, with the source intake verified before
and after execution. The strict replay used Python 3.13.5, NumPy 2.3.5,
SciPy 1.17.0, SymPy 1.14.0 and mpmath 1.3.0, with warnings treated as
errors: all 24 processes completed successfully and their outputs had no
warnings. The [replay receipt](sources/d0_chat_integration_checks_2026-10-28/intake_replay_receipt.json)
records the executable inputs, statuses, output digests and parsed results.
The original CAR script is a diagnostic without its own failure condition;
the wrapper separately requires zero bad quadratic entries, zero trace
difference and two structurally distinct printed eigenvalue expressions with
multiplicity eight. Structural distinction of those expressions is not an
independent proof of unequal numerical eigenvalues. It also prints elapsed
time, so that script's raw stdout digest is run-specific; the replay receipt
retains the digest while comparing its parsed mathematical fields.

A preliminary run on Python 3.9.6 / NumPy 2.0.2 produced runtime warnings
for the clock and secant matrix products, although it exited successfully.
Those two checks were repeated separately on the pinned 3.13.5 environment
with warnings as errors, with empty stderr and outputs byte-equal to the
historical receipts. The preliminary and strict receipts are preserved
separately; their environment-dependent results must not be conflated.
The fresh controls and read-only review do not constitute exhaustive
independent proof of every analytic theorem, scientific acceptance, physical
admission, a full42 replay or a Lean proof.
