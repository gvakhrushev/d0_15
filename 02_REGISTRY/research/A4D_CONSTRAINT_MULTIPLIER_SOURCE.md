## What failed as a compensator

## Other reading

The joint price lives on `D*q_t*D^T=q_s`. Its Lagrangian is

```text
S = B(q,D,b,m) + lambda : (D*q_t*D^T - q_s).
```

Stationarity in `q_s` sets `lambda = dB/dq_s`. A nonzero factor derivative is not a defect to be cancelled by an observer. It is the multiplier of the constraint. Cancelling it would set the multiplier to zero and erase the source.

The phi ladder fixes the ratio of successive AF increments, `u/l=phi^2`, so `2(E1-E2)=-(phi+1)*2*E1` is not a free modulus. The probe identity already says a finished reading is inhabited exactly on `rho0`. The multiplier is the object to compare with `rho0`, not a remainder to kill.
