# Bounded-source coupled boost: spatial compensation needs Cartan scale

Task: `EXP-A4D-JOINT-RESPONSE-DECOUPLING-MICROSTRUCTURE`, Draft PR #310.  
Exact input: [all-amplitude temporal-B current](A4D_COUPLED_BOOST_CURVED_REALIZABILITY.md), Sections 6--8.  
Status: analytic perturbation corollary on a uniform compact link chart; no task terminal.

This revision retains both the connection and the metric change when spatial
links are added. The earlier inequality with only `-C L^4 epsilon` silently
applied the source bound of the corrected field to its uncorrected temporal
template. That step was not valid. The corrected estimate below still
excludes sub-Cartan spatial compensation.

## 1. Exact input and two different fields

Fix the same curved coframe and independently prescribed source:

\[
S_h(x)=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,f(hx_1),f(hx_1)),\quad
f(y)=1+\frac{1-\cos(2\pi y)}{50},\quad h=L^{-1},
\]

with `L in 4*N`. Let T be any collection of temporal links in the one-parameter
group generated by `B=K1+K2+K3`. Denote by `(T,I)` the temporal template and
by `(T,R)` its spatially corrected field, where

\[
R_{x,s}=\exp A_{x,s},\quad s=1,2,3,\qquad
\epsilon=\max_{x,s}\|A_{x,s}\|.
\tag{1}
\]

Assume all temporal links, their inverses, and the spatial interpolation
`exp(sA)`, `0<=s<=1`, lie in a fixed compact chart, uniformly in L.
This is an explicit additional hypothesis for this perturbation argument.
The exact temporal identity itself allows unbounded common rapidities;
a bounded metric response does **not** bound those rapidities. The usual
admissible `||log K||_infinity=O(h)` class satisfies the compactness assumption.

The full corrected field is the one tested against the source:

\[
E_K(S_h,T,R)=0,\qquad \Xi(S_h,T,R)=h^2\tau_h,
\qquad \|\tau_{h,\rm diag}\|_\infty\le M.
\tag{2}
\]

For a putative exact stationary field, Xi is the descended metric readout.
The source is fixed before choosing T or R. The primary vacuum case remains
tau=0. No equation for `Xi(S_h,T,I)` is assumed.

## 2. Transport the source to the temporal template

Analyticity of the literal finite stencil on the uniform compact chart gives
constants C_Q,C_K independent of L and of the fields in that chart such that

\[
\|\Xi_{\rm diag}(S_h,T,I)-\Xi_{\rm diag}(S_h,T,R)\|_\infty
\le C_Q\epsilon,
\tag{3}
\]
\[
\|E_K(S_h,T,I)-E_K(S_h,T,R)\|_{\rm owner,1}
\le C_K L^4\epsilon.
\tag{4}
\]

There are only finitely many incidences per physical site, and the bounded
derivative constants cover the whole interpolation. This proves (3)--(4)
without any smoothness or Fourier assumption on A.

Thus the template obeys only the bound

\[
\|\Xi_{\rm diag}(S_h,T,I)\|_\infty
\le h^2M+C_Q\epsilon=h^2\widehat M,
\qquad \widehat M=M+C_QL^2\epsilon.
\tag{5}
\]

The exact temporal current estimate needs just this response bound; it does
not require assigning any new source to the template. Applying it with
`Mhat`, and then (4), yields

\[
\boxed{
\|E_K(S_h,T,R)\|_{\rm owner,1}
\ge \frac{102}{625}L^3
-90\left(M+C_QL^2\epsilon\right)^2-C_KL^4\epsilon.
}
\tag{6}
\]

The square in (6) is essential. It is the metric-response change of the
template, not an optional higher-order term.

## 3. Sub-Cartan compensation is excluded

If `epsilon=o(h)`, division of (6) by L^3 gives a right side tending to
`102/625>0`: explicitly the possible losses are

\[
90M^2/L^3+180MC_Q\epsilon/L
+90C_Q^2L\epsilon^2+C_KL\epsilon=o(1).
\tag{7}
\]

Hence no exact bounded-source corrected field can have sub-Cartan spatial
links on that compact chart. More quantitatively, choosing any fixed
`c<102/(625 C_K)` (when C_K>0) excludes `epsilon<=c h` on sufficiently fine
meshes, since the metric square in (6), divided by L^3, then vanishes.
This supplies an `Omega(h)` necessary size for spatial compensation.

The conclusion is a whole-field perturbation statement. It makes no
assumption on the temporal amplitude pattern, signs, or derivatives.
Its constants may depend on the declared uniform temporal-link chart;
uniformity over arbitrary unbounded common boosts is not claimed.

## 4. The remaining field must be recentered at the designated connection

Order h cannot be removed by a crude perturbation estimate: the designated
smooth connection itself has logarithm O(h) and cancels the same geometric
current of raw owner size L^3. Any argument at this scale must therefore
retain that transport and study deviations about the exact designated root.

The smooth-critical theorem can be used when **all** rescaled connection
components satisfy its smoothness/precompactness hypotheses, including the
temporal ones. Under those hypotheses it gives the Levi--Civita connection
limit and the Einstein metric response in its stated continuum topology.
Smooth spatial corrections alone do not supply those hypotheses. Likewise,
pointwise or weak Einstein convergence does not automatically imply the
stronger `h^-2 ||Delta Xi||_owner1 -> 0` statement.

For the general O(h) rough deviation on this fixed warp,
[the transverse-mean owner](A4D_WARPED_TRANSVERSE_MEAN_REDUCTION.md) eliminates
the mean variables and states an exact nonlinear response identity with the
raw owner norm retained. It makes no assertion that rough deviations are
already realizable or that their response vanishes.

Verdict: `TEMPORAL-B-SUB-CARTAN-RESCUE-EXCLUDED`, with the uniform compact
chart and source-transfer terms above. No universal Einstein/no-go terminal,
new action, selector, or BOOK/CORE promotion is asserted.
