## D0 input: actual Cl(0,8) carrier

Let L_i, i=1,...,8, be the 16x16 real signed-permutation matrices given by left multiplication by the first eight imaginary sedenion basis units.

They satisfy exactly

L_i L_j + L_j L_i = -2 delta_ij I_16.

All 256 ordered Clifford monomials form a basis of Mat(16,R).

Define normalized D0 Witt operators

a_j^D0 = (1/2)(-L_j + i L_{j+4}),
(a_j^D0)^dag = (1/2)(L_j + i L_{j+4}),

j=1,2,3,4,

so that

{a_i,a_j}=0,
{a_i^dag,a_j^dag}=0,
{a_i,a_j^dag}=delta_ij I_16.

The existing Lean implementation omits the factor 1/2 and therefore has CAR constant 4; for this research brief use the normalized operators above.
