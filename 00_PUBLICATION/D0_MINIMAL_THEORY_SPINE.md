2. **Quantum structure follows.** Catalogue-freedom forces linear superposition (T1), the
   quadratic Born rule in dimension 2 (T2), the `Q₈` role algebra (T3), and a Pisot arrow of

   `K(9,11,13)` is unique under the owned three-zone, `+2`-ladder and Lucas-window hypotheses;
   independent spectral/homological coordinates also recover its zone triple. The remaining
   reduction of the window to M1 is exactly the detector/memory stratification theorem:

   reopens zone `17`. The typed detector layer itself has exactly two primitive profiles

   representation with a two-sided orientation catalogue (T6–T9, T45/N13, T46–T52/N14).
4. **One downstream invariant is reused across sectors.** The edge count `359` is the same object in the metric
   cubic, the α leading term, the Yukawa structure, the Einstein–Hilbert proxy, and the

6. **Five sectors have rank three.** Their field incidence is one rational gravity direction
   plus three independent irrational characters; geometry/mass is the unique duplicated row

7. **Qualitative Yukawa rigidity does not select coefficients.** Every non-scalar
   `a+bQ+cQ²` has the same owned distinction/irrationality profile; a unique selector requires
   new quantitative information (T32/N8).
8. **The mass metric has exactly one modulus.** Physical names, shells and transport roots have
