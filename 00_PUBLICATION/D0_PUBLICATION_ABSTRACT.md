## Compact abstract

D0 is a theorem-level reconstruction programme organized around one admissibility axiom:
**a physical law may not carry a mandatory external catalogue** — minimum description length
applied to laws themselves (M1). It investigates whether a description-length principle can
replace tomographic locality in the operational reconstruction programme (Hardy 2001;
Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti 2011; Masanes–Müller 2011). Under explicit finite hypotheses,
the corpus proves linearity of the catalogue-free mediator, the quadratic Born form in
dimension 2, the `Q₈` role-algebra classification and a Pisot/heat-trace arrow.

Under the explicitly registered three-zone, `+2`-ladder and Lucas-window criteria, the unique
finite scene is the complete tripartite graph `K(9,11,13)`. Its edge count `359` is the
identical downstream object entering the transport/metric cubic, the fine-structure leading
term (`ζ_E(0) = 359`, `ζ_E(−1) = α_top⁻¹`, exact
in ℤ[φ]), the Yukawa structure, the discrete Einstein–Hilbert action proxy, and the
dark-energy window `359/160`. This reuse is not a scene selector: `E=359` has 19 positive
ordered tripartite preimages, while `(V,E)=(33,359)` uniquely recovers `(9,11,13)`. The
scene is over-determined by count data — any pair of `(V,E,T)` fixes it — but the derivation of
the scene window from M1 still has one exact typed frontier: excluding history/order as a
primitive detector capability. Without that detector/memory stratification, a third port
reopens the zone-17 rival. The
golden-ratio content of the electromagnetic dressing is
proved to have exactly one origin — the return defects of a single toral automorphism — by
exhausting the full transport splitting field. The transport group classification

with no external field-theory citation.

The five-sector field incidence is also closed: its character map has rank three and
kernel rank two. Gravity is the rational direction, geometry and mass share the sole duplicated
transport character, and electromagnetism/dark energy carry the two other independent
characters.

Within the mass sector, non-degeneracy and irrationality are proved insufficient to select
Yukawa coefficients: every non-scalar equivariant polynomial has the same owned qualitative
profile, containing an injective rational family. Any unique completion must therefore add a

unable to choose `g`. The remaining mass input is therefore one explicit gap-sensitive scalar

a unique joint completion is proved to require both coefficient-sensitive and gap-sensitive

substitute for the other.
